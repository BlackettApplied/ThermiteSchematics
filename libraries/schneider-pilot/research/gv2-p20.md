# gv2-p20 — Schneider GV2-P20

Decision: **candidate**, for the bare breaker's electrical connections. Historical magnetic-trip calibration remains unresolved.

## Identity and evidence

- **S1:** [Schneider Product data sheet, GV2P20](https://www.se.com/ww/en/product/download-pdf/GV2P20), undated generated sheet, retrieved 2026-09-11; physical pp.1–2 and 11. Saved as `references/gv2p20-current.pdf`. Page 1 identifies a three-pole, rotary, thermal-magnetic motor breaker, adjustable 13–18 A. Visually inspected pp.1 and 11, then enlarged p.11 at 600 DPI (clip 45,115,140,230 PDF points). Its GV2P•• diagram is explicitly included in the exact GV2P20 sheet: terminals **1/L1–2/T1, 3/L2–4/T2, 5/L3–6/T3**, three open poles, common mechanical operation and thermal/magnetic trip elements. No permanent terminal links are depicted. Power terminals are screw clamps. It reports magnetic trip **341 A**.
- **S2:** [Schneider Product data sheet, GV2P20](https://iportal.se.com/Contents/docs/SQD-GV2P20.PDF), **6 December 2017**, pp.1–2; `references/gv2p20.pdf`. Same three-pole identity, rotary knob and 13–18 A range, but magnetic trip **223 A**. AC 50/60 Hz; Ue 690 V is capability, not an assigned circuit voltage. Icu: 100 kA at 230/240 V, 50 kA at 400/415 V, 20 kA at 440 V, 10 kA at 500 V, 4 kA at 690 V (IEC 60947-2). Ics respectively 100%, 50%, 75%, 75%, 100% of Icu. Screw clamps: two conductors, solid 1–6 mm², flexible without ferrule 1.5–6 mm², with ferrule 1–4 mm²; 1.7 N·m.
- **S3:** [Schneider Section 18: Contactors and Starters IEC, 0100CT1501](https://iportal.se.com/Contents/docs/0100CT1501_SEC-18.PDF), **20 June 2017**, printed 18-33 / physical p.33; `references/catalog.pdf`. Exact row in table 18.123 was visually inspected: GV2P20, rotary handle, 13–18 A. Its 450 A column is the maximum upstream fuse/circuit breaker for the listed group motor application, **not magnetic trip current**. Family text states three poles, class 10 thermal overload and instantaneous magnetic trip. Accessories are referenced separately.

The job's GV2-P20 punctuation is retained verbatim in catalog.orderNumber. Manufacturer spelling is GV2P20; treating the hyphen as a presentation separator is supported by the exact remaining code, range and rotary-operation match in the historical catalog. No successor code is substituted. Historical description and manufacturer identity agree.

## Modeling and limits

Complete connection coverage for the bare catalog breaker: six main terminals, no fitted auxiliary block, coil, communication, neutral, shield or PE terminal in the applicable connection schematic. Accessory terminals and surrounding panel bonding are outside this order. Terminal keys preserve diagram markings; no functional pin aliases.

Three contact functions use the existing `breaker` mark and `ganged_with` relations. Open means the illustrated OFF state; this is not an assertion that the installed breaker is OFF. No fixed links: closed poles remain switchable. All terminals are application-dependent, with no universally required external connection asserted. No automatic trip behavior or protective coordination is modeled. Voltage/current capabilities remain notes rather than circuit-intent scalars.

The two exact-order sheets do not explain the 223 A versus 341 A change. Installation-era/date-code evidence or Schneider clarification is required to assign the historical unit's magnetic threshold. This does not change the verified six-terminal topology. No scalar magnetic setting is proposed.

Central fixture, schema/render checks and negative tests are left to the coordinator; no bespoke test project was created.

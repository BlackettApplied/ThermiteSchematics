## Selector and bonding

S3 page 2 section 3[4] resolves the **AUTORESTART** modes. **ON**, the factory setting, enables repeated pulsed restart. **OFF** shuts the supply down for overcurrent, overvoltage or overtemperature; restarting then requires mains disconnection for two minutes. The manual requires at least five minutes disconnected before moving the jumper. These are manufacturer mode descriptions, not an assertion that the synthetic example includes a selected jumper or simulates protection behavior.

The drawing identifies the front jumper opening and ON/OFF setting labels but does not expose a reliable pin count, pin marking or selectable pin-to-pin map. The two setting markers are not evidence of two electrical terminals. Configuration contacts remain unmodeled, so coverage remains **partial** and no permanent mode link is authored. The output-adjust potentiometer and output-OK LED are local controls/indicators, without documented remote signal contacts.

S3 declares safety class I and explicitly depicts PE terminal 1. Its four M4 mounting screws are specified as mechanical fixings; no additional bonding terminal or electrical mounting-screw path is identified. Do not infer panel bonding or PE-to-output-return continuity. Any separate housing-bond connection inventory remains unresolved.

## Ratings and DC-input limitation

S2 specifies 90–265 VAC or 110–300 VDC input, with 5.5 A at 100 VAC and 2.4 A at 230 VAC. S3 specifies AC input and depicts L/N/PE connections without a DC-polarity diagram. Its omission does not erase S2's documented DC range, but it also does not establish polarity independence or a DC connection map. The installed hardware revision remains unverified.

Output is 24 VDC SELV ±1%, adjustable 24–28 V. S3 qualifies 20 A at +60 °C with **UL +40 °C**; 24 A at +40 °C requires 110–265 VAC input. These are whole-output conditions, not separate per-clamp allowances. No nominal-voltage scalar or whole-device AC-only rating is assigned.

Input 2/3 and PE 1 remain required under the powered-device library policy. Repeated output clamps are individually application-dependent. Fixed links are conformance requirements for explicit project jumpers; function membership never joins nets. Shared output policy permits the synthetic fixed-link representation alongside field leads and is not a manufacturer claim about multiple conductors per clamp.

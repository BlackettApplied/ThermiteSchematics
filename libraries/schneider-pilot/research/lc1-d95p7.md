# lc1-d95p7 — Schneider Electric LC1-D95P7 (TeSys D contactor)

**Decision: candidate** — identity, coil code, auxiliary configuration and all
twelve factory terminal markings are established from primary Schneider evidence.

## Primary evidence

Schneider Electric LV catalogue, **Chapter B8 – Contactors** (TeSys D/SK/K/GC…),
retrieved from `iportal2.schneider-electric.com` (resolves to `iportal.se.com`),
126 pp.,. Held in `references/schneider-b8-contactors.pdf`.

1. **Selection table, PDF p.8 (catalogue B8/8), "3-pole contactors – Motor
   control … in category AC-3".** Row for `LC1D95pp`:
   - Rated operational current _in AC-3_ (at 440 V, θ ≤ 60 °C) = **95 A**;
     45 kW at 400 V (motor power varies with voltage across the row).
   - "Instantaneous auxiliary contacts" columns = **1 NO + 1 NC** (integral).
   - "Basic reference, to be completed by adding the control voltage code."
   - D80…D95 connection is by screw-clamp terminals or connectors.

2. **A.c. control-voltage code table, PDF p.8** (voltage row
   `24 42 48 110 115 220 230 240 380 400 415 440 500` → 50/60 Hz codes
   `B7 D7 E7 F7 FE7 M7 P7 U7 Q7 V7 N7 R7 S7`): **P7 = 230 V a.c.** Explicitly
   restated on PDF p.4 ("P7 code = 230 V AC"). The a.c. coil is non-polarised.
   Note: built-in coil suppression is standard only on D115/D150 a.c. coils —
   **not** on D80…D95, so the P7 coil is a plain A1–A2 winding. Suppression
   modules (`LA4D…`) and control-circuit take-off (`LA9D8067`) are separately
   ordered accessories and are out of scope for this reference.

3. **Scheme 127266, PDF p.79 (catalogue B8/79), "TeSys D … 3-pole contactors,
   LC1D09 to D150" — visually inspected (rendered at 500 DPI).** Terminal
   marking:
   - Coil: **A1 – A2**
   - Main poles (NO make, ganged, coil-actuated): **1/L1–2/T1, 3/L2–4/T2, 5/L3–6/T3**
   - Integral auxiliary NO: **13 – 14**
   - Integral auxiliary NC: **21 – 22**
     `LC1D95` lies inside the `LC1D09…D150` range this scheme covers, and the p.8
     selection row independently confirms the 1 NO + 1 NC auxiliary.

## Modeling

- Twelve terminals: `1/L1 2/T1 3/L2 4/T2 5/L3 6/T3` (poles), `13 14` (aux NO),
  `21 22` (aux NC), `A1 A2` (coil). `connectionCoverage: complete`.
- Functions: three NO main contacts, one NO + one NC auxiliary contact, one coil.
  `internal_relations`: coil _actuates_ each contact (mechanical actuation only).
- **No fixed links.** All main/auxiliary contacts are switched and the coil
  actuation is mechanical — none imply a permanent net join.
- **No PE/earth terminal** exists on the scheme; none is fabricated.
- Required connection: coil `A1`/`A2` (the contactor performs no function unless
  the coil is energised). Main-pole and auxiliary terminals are application-
  dependent and are not marked required.
- Ratings are conditional and kept in notes: AC-3 Ie = 95 A is defined at 440 V;
  insulation up to 690 V. No scalar voltage/current is asserted on the main-pole
  or auxiliary terminals (load-circuit voltage is application-dependent). The
  coil terminals carry the assigned 230 V AC (50/60 Hz) supply.

## Unresolved / not asserted

- No printed revision/date code was located inside the B8 PDF; it is cited by
  content and page (undated online edition, retrieved 2026-09-11).
- Screw-clamp wire ranges / tightening torques for the D80…D95 base are not
  modeled (documentation only).
- Coil frequency 50/60 Hz recorded in notes; the coil rating stores 230 V AC
  without a scalar frequency to avoid a misleading single value.

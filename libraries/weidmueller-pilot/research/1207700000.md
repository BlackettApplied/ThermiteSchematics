## Inventory, aliases and continuity

The independently reviewed inventory is **C.1–C.16**, **M.1–M.16**, and aggregate **PE**: 33 modeled endpoints. C denotes a cable screw termination and M its corresponding female mating endpoint. The numbers come from S2; C/M are authoring aliases, not printed side identifiers. Terminal order is logical, without a claimed physical viewing direction or installed orientation.

Define exactly **16 fixed paths**, `C.n–M.n` for n=1…16, representing the fixed contact conductors shown in S2. Project jumpers represent these factory paths; they are not instructions to install field jumpers. No numbered poles join each other or PE. Singleton bus functions and connector metadata do not join physical nets. No phase, polarity or application circuit is assigned.

S1 explicitly specifies a PE screw connection; S2 identifies a conductive PE assembly. Model one aggregate `PE`, without inventing C.PE/M.PE, separately numbered end screws, split-PE links or an automatic hood bond. Individual PE cable, mating and bonding interfaces remain unenumerated, so coverage is **partial**. The MATE metadata lists only the 16 numbered female contacts and describes the separate aggregate PE representation.

**PE is required by explicit library policy** for this model's protective-earth use. This is not attributed to a manufacturer universal-installation instruction. Numbered passive contacts remain application-dependent. Projects may annotate deliberate non-use. External protective bonding must be established by the project; required-terminal presence does not prove upstream bonding. No extra shield, signal, auxiliary or communication endpoint is inferred.

## Connection model and limits of the evidence

The drawing establishes 24 individual fixed contact conductors. Preserve the manufacturer numbers. `C.n` denotes the cable screw termination and `M.n` the corresponding female mating endpoint; C/M are authoring aliases, not printed manufacturer legends. Define exactly 24 explicit fixed paths `C.n-M.n`, n=1..24. Distinct numbered contacts stay electrically separate, including separation from PE. No phase map, application wiring or mirrored installed orientation is inferred.

PE existence is no longer uncertain: S1 explicitly specifies its screw connection and S2 identifies its conductive assembly. Use one aggregate `PE` endpoint. Do not invent split cable/mating PE sides, separately numbered end screws or an automatic hood bond. The individual interfaces of the PE assembly remain unenumerated, so coverage is **partial**, even though the numbered contact inventory is complete. The MATE metadata enumerates only the 24 numbered female contacts and points readers to the separate aggregate PE representation.

The aggregate PE endpoint is marked **required** as an explicit library policy for the connector's modeled protective-earth use, rather than an attributed manufacturer instruction for every possible application. Numbered passive circuits remain application-dependent. Projects may annotate deliberate non-use and must establish the actual external protective bond. Required-terminal presence does not prove upstream bonding. No extra shield, dedicated signal, auxiliary or communication contact is added. The manufacturer classifies all 24 numbered contacts as power contacts; their eventual electrical uses are not selected here.

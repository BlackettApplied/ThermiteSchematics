# gv3-a02 — Schneider Electric GV3-A02

Decision: **candidate**. Preserve type ID `schneider-pilot:gv3-a02` and order `GV3-A02`. The historical Schneider diagram prints this exact hyphenated code; the later product sheet spells it `GV3A02`. Both identify the same GV3 auxiliary-contact configuration.

## Evidence

- **S1:** [Schneider Electric, Product data sheet: GV3A02, TeSys GV3 auxiliary contact, 2 NO early-break](https://iportal.se.com/Contents/docs/SQD-GV3A02.PDF), September 1, 2016, p.1. Manufacturer-hosted; saved as `references/gv3a02-datasheet.pdf`. Establishes identity, GV3ME compatibility, two NO contacts, right mounting and screw clamps. Full page visually inspected. Photograph appears to show 21/22 alongside 13/14, inconsistent with its 2-NO text; do not derive numbering from this illustrative photograph.
- **S2:** [Schneider Electric / Telemecanique, TeSys circuit-breakers: thermal-magnetic motor circuit-breakers type GV3-ME](https://www.elmatik.ee/info/pdf/GV3.pdf), undated historical excerpt, printed 3/58, 3/60, 3/62 (PDF 7, 9, 11). Manufacturer-authored at Elmatik mirror; saved as `references/historical-gv3.pdf`. Visually inspected full pages and enlarged exact GV3-A02 diagram on 3/62: NO 13–14 and NO 23–24, dashed mechanical linkage. Operation chart on 3/58 shows both open at OFF and closed at ON. Ordering table 3/60 instead prints GV1-A02; this discrepancy is recorded, not silently corrected. Exact GV3-A02 scheme and S1 establish the selected identity independently of that row.

## Connection and model scope

Four screw-clamp terminals: **13, 14, 23, 24**. Complete connection coverage for this passive block; no supply, return, PE, shield or communications connection is documented or modeled. No spare terminals are shown for the exact order. Each terminal is application-dependent (`required: false`). Two `contact-no` functions, mechanically ganged, represent position operation; no fault-only signaling function. No factory fixed links: switched contact paths never permanently join nets.

Early-break behavior is preserved in notes; the schema cannot encode travel/timing relative to the separately modeled host breaker. No physical top/bottom orientation is inferred.

S1 ratings: Ui 690 V IEC / 600 V UL and CSA; Ith 6 A; Ue 48–690 V AC or 24–220 V DC. At 100,000 operations, AC-11 power is 350/500/800/850/700/700/400 VA at respectively 48/110–127/220–240/380–415/440/500/690 V; DC-11 is 180/240/180/140/120 W at 24/48/60/110/220 V. These conditional capabilities remain notes, with no assigned nominal voltage or scalar switching-current limit. S1 specifies one/two solid conductors 1–2.5 mm²; flexible 0.75–2.5 mm², except two ferruled conductors limited to 0.75–1.5 mm².

## Connection inventory and model

The exact drawing supports **two intended electrical blade interfaces**, circuit labels **1** and **2**. Preserve those labels without claiming that they are stamped on the blades or establish physical top/bottom, supply/load, or polarity. The dimension/end views distinguish the blades from the handling hardware. The integral front indicator has no separate wired contact in these drawings; no remote-indication terminal is invented. Live grip lugs and assembly screws are not PE or additional wiring interfaces. The holder/base and external accessories are separate components.

The fuse is represented by one normally closed `contact` function mapped to the existing `fuse` symbol. That is an intact-fuse drawing convention, not a trip simulation or permanent conductor. **Fixed links: none. Required terminal keys: none**; use of the passive fuse link is application-dependent. No PE, shield or communication interface is introduced. Coverage is complete for this standalone fuse-link scope.

## Ratings and conditions

S1 pp.1–2 specifies **NH000**, **gG**, **25 A rated AC current**, **500 V AC / 250 V DC**, **21 mm** body width and **2.3 W** hot loss per pole at rated AC current. It lists **23.75 A at 40°C**. S2 additionally gives **24.5 / 23.75 / 23 / 22.5 / 22 / 21.5 A** at **30 / 40 / 45 / 50 / 55 / 60°C** respectively. The separately declared operating range is **−5 to +40°C**; higher-temperature current entries are not permission to extend that range. No capability is encoded as `nominal_voltage` or an unconditional terminal-current limit.

The historical sheets give **25 kA DC** and a separate **120 kA** switching-capacity entry under **IEC 60947-2**. Preserve the source wording and conditions; the latter field does not itself repeat a voltage type, and this candidate does not independently establish protective coordination or time-current/let-through performance. **IP20** applies **with connected conductors**. The environmental category **−20 to +50 at 95% relative humidity** is distinct from the operating-ambient range. Mounting is any position, preferably vertical.

Its gL/gG wording is retained in the source inventory; this model uses Siemens' explicit **gG** classification. Installed revision, fuse-base suitability and application protection design remain project responsibilities.

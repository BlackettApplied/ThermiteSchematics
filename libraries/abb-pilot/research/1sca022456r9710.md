## Proposed scope

For each of three schematic poles, the two external cable endpoints and two fuse-seat endpoints are represented separately. `P1/P2/P3`, `EXT`, `FUSE`, and `A/B` are **authoring aliases only**. They assign no ABB terminal inscription, physical left/right or top/bottom position, line/load orientation, or phase identity.

Each pole has two separate contact functions: `EXT.A–FUSE.A` and `EXT.B–FUSE.B`, shown open as in ABB's schematic and ganged to the common mechanism. The removable fuse would connect `FUSE.A–FUSE.B`; its identity, rating and characteristic are unestablished and it is not represented as fitted hardware. There are **no fixed links**. Six contact symbols preserve the two-sided switching arrangement without shorting a switch or fuse. Required-terminal coverage is empty because passive pole use is application-dependent.

Coverage stays **partial**: manufacturer terminal marks, physical mapping, clamp subdivisions and an exhaustive installed-assembly inventory remain unresolved. No inferred auxiliary, fourth pole, PE terminal, panel bond or optional accessory is added. This abstraction is suitable for reviewing endpoint roles, not determining an installation from aliases.

The previous hold's essential functional topology gap is recovered; the physical identification gap remains explicit. A parent review of the diagram and generated circuit is still required. No central tests or canonical edits were performed.

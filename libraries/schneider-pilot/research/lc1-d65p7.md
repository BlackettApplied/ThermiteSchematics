# lc1-d65p7 — Schneider LC1-D65P7

Decision: candidate, with partial physical connection coverage.

## Historical exact evidence

Coordinator reviewed the exact historical LC1-D65•• row and P7 table in manufacturer TeSys catalogue 24501-EN_Ver6.0 p.24501/2 (physical 1), with the applicable LC1-D09–D150 circuit 24532/2 (physical 13). LC1-D65P7 is established directly; newer LC1D65A ratings/terminal hardware are not transferred. The historical row gives 65 A AC-3, 18.5 kW at 220/230 V, 30 kW at 380/400 V and 37 kW at 415/440/500 V. P7 is 230 VAC 50/60 Hz. Three NO main poles plus NO 13/NO–14 and NC 21/NC–22 auxiliary and A1/A2 coil; no built-in suppressor is specified for this historical AC D65.

[Manufacturer catalogue hosted by CERN](https://sy-dep-epc-lpc.web.cern.ch/components/datasheets/epc-lpc%20%28converters%29/LC1-D_Breaker%20Contactor_Telemecanique.pdf). The coordinator visually reviewed physical pages 1 and 13; local original is retained under lc1-d65p7/references/historical.pdf with rendered pages.

## Connection model

Main NO pairs 1/L1–T1/2,3/L2–T2/4,5/L3–T3/6 preserve the historical diagram punctuation. Auxiliary NO 13–14 and NC 21–22, coil A1–A2: 12 labeled endpoints total. Required coil 230 VAC 50/60 Hz; passive poles remain application-dependent. Mechanical actuation creates no fixed net links. Contact capability values stay in notes rather than assigned per-terminal current or voltage. No additional PE, shield or communication endpoint is documented.

## Coordinator correction

The original worker used a newer LC1D65A row and described the historical type as merely a terminal-technology variant. This does not establish matching historical ratings. The exact older LC1-D65 row has 30 kW at 380/400 V versus the newer 37 kW claim; the corrected record uses the older row and preserves physical terminal marks. Original worker proposal remains private for traceability.

Physical duplicate-access/clamping positions and any accessory suppression attachment geometry are not separately enumerated for the historical hardware edition; connection coverage is partial for those details.

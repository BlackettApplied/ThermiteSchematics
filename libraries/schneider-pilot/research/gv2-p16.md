# gv2-p16 — Schneider GV2-P16 (manufacturer spelling GV2P16)

**Decision: candidate.** Identity, ratings and the full connection inventory are
established from a manufacturer datasheet plus a visually inspected connection
diagram.

## Identity

- Job order `GV2-P16` = manufacturer catalog spelling **GV2P16** (no separator;
  the hyphen is a presentation separator, not a different part). Not a successor substitution.- Product: Schneider Electric **TeSys GV2** (TeSys Deca) **thermal-magnetic motor
  circuit breaker** (manual motor starter / protector), 3-pole, one compact unit
  combining manual disconnect + thermal overload + instantaneous magnetic trip.
- Control: **rotary knob / rotary handle**; **screw-clamp** terminals.

## Ratings (manufacturer datasheet + catalogue)

- Rated current In / Ith = **14 A**; **thermal overload adjustment 9…14 A**.
- **Magnetic trip fixed at 170 A (±20 %)**, non-adjustable.
- Ue = **690 V AC** 50/60 Hz; Ui 690 V; Uimp 6 kV. **AC only**, utilisation AC-3 /
  Category A (IEC 60947-2 / 60947-4-1). Phase-failure sensitive; suitable for
  isolation.
- Icu: 100 kA @230/240 V, 100 kA @400/415 V, 50 kA @440 V, 42 kA @500 V, 6 kA @690 V.
- Motor power: 5.5 kW @400/415 V, 7.5 kW @500 V, 9 kW @690 V (11 kW @690 V per
  datasheet). Max fuse/CB 450 A (US Digest).
- Screw-clamp terminals, tightening torque **1.7 N·m**; W45×H89×D97 mm; IP20.

Ratings are capabilities / adjustment bounds, not an assigned circuit voltage or
installed trip setting.

## Connection inventory (diagram reviewed)

Catalogue schematic **DF510744** (TeSys GV Catalogue 2017, page 9) inspected at
600 DPI: three poles, each with a thermal element and a magnetic (`I>`) element,
all **mechanically linked** by the common operating mechanism / trip.

| Pole | Line (top) | Load (bottom) |
| ---- | ---------- | ------------- |
| 1    | 1/L1       | 2/T1          |
| 2    | 3/L2       | 4/T2          |
| 3    | 5/L3       | 6/T3          |

- **Six main power terminals only.** No protective-earth terminal exists on the
  device (plastic-housed DIN-rail / panel unit, fixed by clip or 2×M4 screws).
- **No integral auxiliary / signaling contact.** Auxiliary contact blocks
  (GVAE / GVAD / GVAN), undervoltage / shunt releases, and the GV2AK00 visible-
  isolation block are separately-ordered add-ons with their own terminals and are
  **not part of GV2-P16**; none is modeled.
- The three poles are **ganged (mechanical linkage only)** — modeled as
  `internal_relations: ganged_with`, which is **not** an electrical net join.
  Breaker poles are switching contacts, so **no fixedLinks** are declared.

Modeled as 6 terminals, 3 contact poles, `renderKind: circuit`, `breaker` symbol
per pole. `connectionCoverage: complete` — the bare device's physical connection
set is exactly these 6 terminals.

## Operator naming: Europe vs US (resolved)

The shared **US Digest section-18** Table18.123 agrees with the European sources: GV2P has a rotary handle and GV2ME has push buttons. The coordinator corrected an initial column-alignment reading error.

## Remaining / caveats

  range (low risk).

  documentation, not modeled or verified here.

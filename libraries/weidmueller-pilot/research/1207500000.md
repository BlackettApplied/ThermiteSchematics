## Corrections and topology

1. Replace the unsupported “central PE” inventory with **one aggregate PE authoring endpoint**, explicitly partial. The manufacturer supports the PE assembly and its screw connection, but this model does not claim a count of physical clamps or mating areas. No additional housing earth screw is invented.
2. Preserve **C.1-C.16** and **M.1-M.16** using the exact drawing's numbered identities. C/M are logical side aliases. Sixteen explicit project jumpers C.n-M.n represent fixed contact continuity; singleton functions and port metadata never create nets. Remove the speculative C.PE-M.PE path. The model consequently has **33 authoring endpoints and 16 fixed links**, not a claim of 33 physical pins.
3. Mark aggregate **PE required:true** as explicit library policy for modeled protective-earth use, not a universal manufacturer quotation or an obligation to wire every visible screw. Numbered contacts are application-dependent. Shared PE authoring does not authorize multiple field conductors in one clamp or prove protective bonding.
4. Remove **nominal_voltage:500** from every numbered terminal and do not add an AC-only voltage_type. The compiler treats nominal_voltage as exact compatibility (E302), so a documentary capability bound would wrongly reject lower-voltage applications. Keep 500 V DIN EN 61984 and 600 V AC/DC UL/CSA in notes, and retain 16 A current capability with derating/approval qualifications.
5. Keep the terminal/earth renderer mappings, and describe connectorPorts.MATE.PE as an aggregate assembly interface rather than a single pin. Maintain partial coverage for the unenumerated physical PE positions and installed orientation.

The exact datasheet also distinguishes main conductors **0.5-2.5 mm2, M3, 9 mm strip** from PE **4 mm2 rated cross-section, AWG20-12, M4, 10 mm strip**. Do not apply main-contact limits to PE or promote one dated torque range as universally applicable to installed legacy hardware.

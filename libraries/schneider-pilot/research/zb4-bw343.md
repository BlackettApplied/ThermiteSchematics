## Modeling decision — mechanical head, zero electrical terminals

- **Scope:** ZB4BW343 is the **electrically passive mechanical head sub-assembly** only
  (chromium-plated metal bezel, round, flush, red, plain lens, spring return, Ø22.5 mm,
  for universal-LED bodies). It is **not** a complete illuminated pushbutton and **not** a
  contact block or light block.
- **Terminal inventory:** **zero** documented electrical terminals. No contacts, no LED,
  no light-block terminals, no supply/return/PE terminal. Contacts, the LED and all
  wiring terminals are provided by the separately ordered body sub-assembly (ZB4BW0…) and
  are out of scope. No contacts/LED were imported into the head from the complete-unit
  table (per hint).
- **Bonding/attachment:** the metal bezel mounts in a Ø22.5 mm cutout and snap-fits to a
  ZB4B body. The datasheet documents no PE/bond terminal on the head; any earth path
  through the panel/collar/metalwork is an installation matter, not a manufacturer-
  documented terminal. Modeled as **partial** coverage with zero documented terminals; no
  bonding path asserted as manufacturer fact.
- **Render:** zero-terminal mechanical item → documentation/accessory view
  (`renderKind: accessory`); no invented wiring, no circuit symbols, no fixed links.
- **Diagram review:** I visually inspected catalog p.31 (heads-only table, no contact
  columns) and p.30 (the NO/NC scheme, which applies to the body/complete unit) to confirm
  the head carries no contacts. There is no terminal/connection diagram for the head
  itself because it has none.

## Decision

**candidate.** Identity and electrical scope are established by the manufacturer datasheet
and catalog: a red flush illuminated-pushbutton head with zero electrical terminals.
Conformance is an empty terminal inventory (accessory), coverage partial.

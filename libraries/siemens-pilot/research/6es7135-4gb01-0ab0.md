## Accepted model

- Assembled field-interface abstraction on documented TM-E15C24-01 (6ES7193-4CB30-0AA0): eight field clamps, no AUX clamps. Card and base are separately ordered. Verify the installed per-slot pairing before use. Do not duplicate these field clamps with a second base instance in the same circuit.
- P1=L+ and P2=1M are the power-bus supply identities in Figure 1-1, not additional field screw terminals. BACKPLANE is an authoring alias for the unenumerated ET 200S bus interface. Its physical contact count and pinout are not modeled.
- Exact p.7 terminal table and p.8 compatible-base diagram document eight numbered clamps: 1=QI0, 5=QI1; 3/7=MANA; 2/6/4/8=n.c. AUX1 field clamps occur on other base variants, not the selected 24-01.
- Figure 1-1 directly joins 3 and 7 (MANA): represent by explicit project jumper. Neither channel metadata nor supply function creates continuity. P2/1M is isolated from MANA; no output-to-return permanent link is allowed.
- Load supply is nominal 24 VDC from the power module, maximum module consumption 150 mA. Signal capability is ±20 mA or 4–20 mA into maximum 500 ohms; signal ratings are retained here without assigning output voltage or a misleading scalar current limit.
- Channels can be deactivated (p.11); numbered terminals are not universally required. Supply P1/P2 are required. Shared policy for 3/7 supports the explicit fixed-link representation and is not a clamp wire-count approval.
- Shielded twisted-pair wiring is recommended; p.19 distinguishes grounding both ends from one-end grounding where endpoint potential differences occur. External shield/bonding hardware is outside the electronic-card inventory.
- Partial physical inventory: numbered clamps belong to the selected separate base; P1/P2 identify internal functional supplies, not every mating finger. Backplane contacts, actual per-slot pairing and external shield/bonding hardware remain unverified. No additional PE or AUX bond is inferred.

The fixed links are explicit source jumpers in the example; functions and metadata do not create continuity. Module capability ratings do not establish conductor sizing or protective coordination.

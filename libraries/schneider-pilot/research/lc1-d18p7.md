# lc1-d18p7 — Schneider Electric LC1-D18P7 (TeSys D contactor)

## Identity

- Job identity: `schneider-pilot:lc1-d18p7`, manufacturer **Schneider Electric**,
  order **LC1-D18P7**.- Manufacturer spelling is **LC1D18P7** (no hyphen). The order code is composed
  as basic reference `LC1D18` + a.c. control-voltage code `P7`. The job's hyphen
  in `LC1-D18P7` is a presentation separator, not a distinct catalog spelling.
  This is the exact part, not a successor; no substitution was made.

## Primary evidence

Both sources are Schneider Electric authored/hosted (authority: manufacturer).

1. **B8 Contactors catalogue** (Schneider iPortal), fetched to
   `references/schneider-b8-contactors.pdf` (, 126 pp).
   - **PDF p.8 (B8/8)** — 3-pole AC-3 selection table. Row `LC1D18pp`: rated
     AC-3 operational current **18 A**, standard motor rating **7.5 kW at 400 V**
     (9 kW at 440/500 V), **1 + 1 instantaneous auxiliary contacts** (1 NO + 1 NC),
     "Connection by screw clamp terminals", basic reference "to be completed by
     adding the control voltage code". Rated operational voltage Ue up to 690 V
     (table header "230 V 400 V … 690 V").
   - Same page, **Standard control circuit voltages, a.c. supply** (LC1D09…D150):
     Volts row `24 42 48 110 115 220 230 240 380 400 415 440 500` aligned to code
     row (50/60 Hz) `B7 D7 E7 F7 FE7 M7 P7 U7 Q7 V7 N7 R7 S7`. **P7 = 230 V AC,
     50/60 Hz** (also stated verbatim elsewhere: "(P7 code = 230 V AC)", PDF p.7).
   - Suppression note: for a.c. coils only **D115 and D150** have built-in
     suppression; integral suppression is otherwise a **DC-coil** feature
     (LC1D09…D38 DC codes). Therefore the **P7 (a.c.) coil of LC1D18 has no
     integrated suppressor** — no internal component and no fabricated net.
   - **PDF p.79 (B8/79)** — Schemes, drawing **127266.eps**, "TeSys D, TeSys D
     Green 3-pole contactors … LC1D09 to D150". **Visually inspected** at 600 DPI
     (`references/schneider-b8-contactors-page-79-clip-5_148_180_220-600dpi.png`):
     - Coil **A1–A2** (rectangle).
     - Three NO power poles: **1/L1–2/T1**, **3/L2–4/T2**, **5/L3–6/T3**.
     - Built-in aux **NO 13/NO–14** and **NC 21/NC–22**.
       The dashed line is the mechanical actuation link (coil drives all contacts);
       it is not an electrical net.

2. **Schneider Electric product page** `se.com/us …/LC1D18P7/` — corroborates the

- All three power poles and both auxiliary contacts are **mechanically actuated
  by the coil** (`internal_relations: actuates`). No permanent conductor joins any
  terminals: **`fixedLinks: []`**. Switched poles/contacts and the (absent)
  suppressor never imply net joins.
- **No PE/earth terminal.** A base 3-pole TeSys D contactor has no protective-
  conductor terminal (earth-screen-continuity terminals belong to specific
  dust/damp add-on aux blocks, which are separately ordered and not part of this
  part). The published circuit has no PE terminal; physical access scope is qualified below.
- **Required:** only the coil terminals **A1, A2** are marked required (the
  contactor cannot operate without coil energisation). Power poles and passive
  auxiliary contacts are application-dependent and not marked universally required.

## Ratings (documentation, not scalar checks)

- Coil: **230 V AC, 50/60 Hz** (code P7). Encoded on A1/A2 as nominal_voltage
  230, voltage_type AC (matches source circuit intent). 50/60 Hz kept as a note
  (frequency is a dual value, not modeled as a scalar).
- Power poles: AC-3 **Ie = 18 A**, Ue up to **690 V**; 7.5 kW/400 V. TeSys D poles
  are rated for AC and DC utilisation categories; conditional AC-1/AC-3/DC tables
  are not flattened to a scalar, and no AC-only metadata is assigned to the poles.
- Auxiliary contacts: standard TeSys D thermal current Ith 10 A, AC-15/DC-13
  ratings apply (values not required for terminal identity; left as documentation).

## Unresolved / out of scope

- Exact auxiliary-contact utilisation-category limits (AC-15/DC-13), power-pole

## Decision

**candidate.** Exact identity, coil suffix/supply, all three main NO poles, both
factory auxiliary contacts (1 NO + 1 NC) and every terminal mark are established
from the manufacturer selection table and the visually inspected scheme
127266.eps. Connection coverage: partial. No fixed links.

## Connection model

Four terminals: **1,2,3,4**. Two protected, mechanically ganged, interruptible paths: **1–2** and **3–4**. The diagram's dashed coupling is mechanical, not a conductor. **No fixed links** are authored. Two contact functions use the existing breaker symbol, with open state representing the illustrated OFF position. No phase/neutral names, supply direction or operating-state automation is inferred.

Coverage is **complete for the bare breaker**. Separate trip-coil, auxiliary-contact and residual-current accessories are excluded, as are invented PE/shield/communication terminals. No individual terminal is universally required for every application. Shared connection policy does not establish physical multi-conductor clamp capacity.

## Conditional ratings

Rated current **2 A at 30°C**; curve C magnetic operation **5–10 × In**. For the **2P** table: **Icn 6000 A at 400 V AC, CEI EN 60898**; **Icu 20 kA at 230–240 V AC or 10 kA at 400–415 V AC, CEI EN 60947-2**. Keep those conditions separate. **Ui500 V AC**, **Uimp6 kV** and stated **Ue400 V AC** are capabilities, not assigned installation voltage. No `nominal_voltage` scalar is used.

At In≤25 A, cage-terminal capacity is up to **16 mm² flexible / 25 mm² rigid** conductor. No wire size, torque, DC rating or installation suitability is selected. Thermal trip timing, protection coordination and fitted hardware revision remain outside the model.

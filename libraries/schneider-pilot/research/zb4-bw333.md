# Schneider ZB4-BW333 — zb4-bw333

Decision: **candidate**, limited to the mechanical head; connection coverage **partial**.

Identity is supported by the historical Schneider/Telemecanique selection row in S2, which explicitly prints **ZB4-BW333**, and the corresponding modern row in S3, printed **ZB4BW333**. Both identify a green flush head for an integral-LED body, weighing 0.026 kg; their matching illustration identifier is 101244. This supports treating the hyphen as catalog presentation while retaining the job's exact order number. The source's “round countersunk” wording corresponds to the manufacturer's flush operator description; “lighted pushbutton sub-assembly” must not be expanded into a complete electrical assembly.

Evidence opened and reviewed:

- **S1:** [Schneider Product data sheet, ZB4BW333](https://iportal.se.com/Contents/docs/SQD-ZB4BW333.PDF), dated November 11, 2015; physical/printed pp. 1, 3, 7–8. Saved as `references/zb4bw333.pdf`. Visually inspected the characteristics, head dimensions and composition diagrams/legend. The sheet identifies a round, green, unmarked, plain-lens, spring-return head, chromium-plated metal bezel, 22 mm mounting diameter. Class I is stated. Composition codes describe compatible arrangements of separately fitted blocks, not included contacts. No head terminal or bonding designation is shown. No supply voltage or switching-current rating is assigned to this mechanical model.
- **S2:** [Unidades de mando y señalización — Telemecanique Harmony Style 4](https://daks2k3a4ib2z.cloudfront.net/5762dae4eefe322819722485/598480547e663500010f7a1b_Unidades_mando_senalizacion_CI.pdf), undated historical Schneider-authored catalog excerpt at a CloudFront mirror. Physical p. 7, printed p. 5/329; saved as `references/historical-harmony.pdf`. Visually inspected the exact head row, associated round/flush illustration and labeled product image. It restricts this head to a body containing an integral-LED light block. The head is separately identified from the body.
- **S3:** [Harmony XB4 metal control and signaling units catalog](https://iportal.se.com/Contents/docs/HARMONY%20XB4%20METAL%20CONTROL%20AND%20SIGNALING%20UNITS_CATALOG.PDF), October 2021; supplied local `references/schneider-xb4-2021.pdf`, physical p. 33 / printed p. 31. Visually inspected exact ZB4BW333 selection row and illustration. It lists heads only with plain lens; footnote 2 locates the integral LED in the associated body.

Connection inventory established independently of the model: no documented power, return, signal, communication, shield or PE terminal on the selected head; no contact or light block included; no documented fixed electrical link. Thus conformance has empty terminal, required-terminal and fixed-link lists. This is not an assertion that metalwork never requires bonding. S1's Class I classification does not identify a bonding terminal or prove continuity through a collar or panel. That attachment/bonding interface remains unresolved, and coverage is partial accordingly.

The proposal uses an accessory documentation view, empty electrical functions and no invented circuit symbol, connector pin, contact, LED supply or continuity. Electrical blocks and mounting-body details must be established separately for any assembled device.

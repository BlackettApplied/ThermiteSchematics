The source has eight side-qualified endpoints representing four cable-side poles
and four mating contacts. `C` and `M` are authoring prefixes. The manufacturer
four-contact drawing supplies L1/L2/L3 contact identities, not an exact 7h
installation view. Cable-clamp legends and individual screw/bond positions have
not been verified. No numeric pin identities or correspondence to the neutral
`P1`/`P2` aliases used by the separate 230 V three-contact model are introduced.

Manufacturer contact identities do not establish a numerical pin alias. Verify
actual markings before assigning installation wiring.

`MATE` maps L1/L2/L3/PE to `M.L1`/`M.L2`/`M.L3`/`M.PE`. This is an identity map,
not a coordinate map, phase-sequence declaration or mating approval. The model
retains `connectionCoverage: partial` because exact 7h orientation, rear/cable
markings and the screw inventory remain open.

The exact base selection has no fitted auxiliary contact terminals. Optional
accessory seats are mechanical provisions, not additional electrical endpoints.
No neutral, shield, communication, signal terminal or additional bonding screw
is established for this selected base by the reviewed evidence. Accessory-equipped
or differently suffixed selections require their own part review.

## Functions, capability and limits

Three `contact` functions use `switch-no` symbols and ganging relations; open
means the OFF reference position. Separate single-terminal PE `bus` functions use
terminal symbols. These functions and `MATE` metadata never join physical nets.
The three switched pole pairs must not receive permanent internal jumpers.
The example authors only the fixed `C.PE`-`M.PE` link.

Each power endpoint carries a 16 A AC capability and 500 V range upper bound.
This is not an assigned circuit voltage; no 690 V family rating, DC capability,
wire size, load power or protective-device selection is silently transferred.

The catalog gives the base -30 to +40 degrees C, while the older manual presents
a wider family range. Neither ambient suitability nor IP66/Ex suitability is an
automatic compiler decision. Installed revisions, assembly condition, seals,
entries, upstream protection and application require review. The manual's stated
plug-family restriction does not approve every member or a modern replacement.

`C.PE` is universally required. `shared` allows the explicit internal link and an
external lead in the model; it does not approve two field conductors in a clamp.
Connection presence cannot establish an external protective-earth supply: an
isolated internal jumper already occupies both PE endpoints. The example tests
that limitation explicitly. Mating contacts may remain unused with no plug fitted.

on Node 24.18.0, Apple Silicon macOS, with the existing source build. It preserves
three W904 warnings: the socket and its two synthetic boundaries remain partial.
Checks cover all eight endpoints and port identities; three switched functions;
all pairwise pole isolation; explicit PE continuity and its removal; missing
required PE; a floating internal PE link; all five functions and nine conductors
in one rendered circuit view; and E108 rejection after a library-byte change.
The rendered tabloid sheet was visually inspected for conductor completeness,
labels and clipping. The shared example is locked to the pilot library bytes;
ordinary verification also checks that integrated lock.

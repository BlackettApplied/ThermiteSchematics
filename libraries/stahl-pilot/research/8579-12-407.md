# 8579-12-407 — R. STAHL 8579/12-407 (SolConeX plug, 63 A)

- **Historical description (evidence, not spec):** "3 Pole + PE plug", "Plug male 63A,
  480-500V, 50-60Hz, 7h", "Colour black".
- **Decision:** `candidate`. Exact identity, ratings, four-contact terminal diagram and

## Identity and ratings — confirmed against primary sources

R. STAHL single data sheet, **Plug 63 A, 8579/12-407, Art. No. 153037** (2022-10-05, V 0.98 EN):

- Order number `8579/12-407`, SolConeX series, manufacturer R. STAHL Schaltgeräte GmbH.
- Rated operational voltage **480 to 500 V AC**; rated operational current **max. 63 A**;
  frequency **50 / 60 Hz**.
- **Detailed number of poles: 3 P + PE** (4-pole); **Colour code: Black**;
  **Coding (clock hour position): 7**.
- **Connection: screw terminal**, one finely-stranded conductor **2.5 – 16 mm²** (AWG 14–6).
- Polyamide enclosure, **IP66**, impact 7 J / IK10; ATEX/IECEx **Ex db eb IIC T6…T5 Gb /
  Ex tb IIIC**, Zones 1/2/21/22. Ambient −30…+40 °C (T6) / −30…+55 °C (T5).
- Accessory: **Protective cap for 63 A 4-/5-pole plugs, Art. No. 153261** (separate mechanical
  part, outside this type).

Every element of the historical description matches the exact `-407` selection.

## Modeling rationale

- **Plug = fixed contact conductors, not switched poles.** Unlike the interlocked wall socket,
  each cable-side screw terminal is permanently continuous with its mating pin. Modeled as four
  cable-side endpoints `C.L1/C.L2/C.L3/C.PE` and four mating pins `M.L1/M.L2/M.L3/M.PE`, joined
  by four factory **fixed links** (`conformance.fixedLinks`). `C`/`M` are authoring-side aliases;
  `L1/L2/L3` are manufacturer contact identities; `PE` is a textual alias for the ⏚ symbol.
- **PE:** `C.PE` is `required`; the manual mandates connecting the protective conductor. The
  required-PE check is satisfied by the internal `C.PE-M.PE` link and does **not** assert an
  external protective-earth supply.
- **Ratings:** terminal rating uses 500 V (the upper bound of the `-407` 480–500 V arrangement)
  as a capability bound, not an authored supply voltage; the series 690 V/750 V figures are the
  higher family capability, not the `-407` assignment.
- **Coverage `partial`:** three power contacts + one PE only. No neutral on this `-407`
  (the 5-pole `-5..` adds N), no shield/signal/communication/auxiliary contact or extra bond screw.
- **Render:** `circuit` — eight single-terminal bus functions, each drawn as a `terminal`.

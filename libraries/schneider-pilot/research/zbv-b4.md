# zbv-b4 — Schneider ZBV-B4

Decision: **candidate**. Model `schneider-pilot:zbv-b4`, manufacturer **Schneider Electric**, order **ZBV-B4**, as the separately ordered red integral LED light block for 22 mm Harmony XB4/XB5 heads.

Schneider's exact product sheet uses ZBVB4; its historical selection table uses ZBV B4. The matching code characters, red LED, 24 V AC/DC and 22 mm application support treating the separator as presentation punctuation. Preserve the job spelling in the model. “Indicator” in the source is broader than the manufacturer's light-block scope: a head, fixing collar, switch contacts, transformer and test-light accessory are not included.

Connection specification: two external lamp supply terminals, X1 and X2. Both are required by the job's lamp policy. Use neutral supply roles without assigning DC polarity from the ZBVB1 FAQ. The light block has one load function rendered by the supported `lamp` mark. X1 and X2 are distinct electrical nets: the LED load is not a fixed conductor, so factory fixedLinks is empty. Complete coverage applies to the bare light block: no switch, PE/bond, shield or communication connection is identified or transferred from surrounding equipment. Collar/panel bonding belongs to a separately specified assembly; no attachment bond is asserted.

Model ratings remain notes, including the 24 V nominal supply and separate AC/DC ranges; no AC-only metadata or disputed current scalar is assigned. Shared clamp policy reflects the documented two-conductor capacity.

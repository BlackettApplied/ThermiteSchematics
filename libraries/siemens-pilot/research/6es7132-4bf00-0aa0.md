## Accepted modeling scope

- Exact electronic module represented at the documented TM-E15C24-01 (6ES7193-4CB30-0AA0) assembled field interface: eight numbered field clamps and no AUX clamps. Card and base are separately ordered. Verify the installed per-slot pairing before use. Do not add this abstraction and a second full field-clamp representation to the same circuit.
- Page 7 assigns 1=DO0, 5=DO1, 2=DO2, 6=DO3, 3=DO4, 7=DO5, 4=DO6, 8=DO7. Channels are application-dependent, so no output is universally required.
- Page 9 identifies internal power-bus contacts P1=1M and P2=L+. These are supply interface endpoints, not additional field screw terminals. Both are required for powered operation; explicit project connections must represent their supply through the separate power/terminal modules.
- Rated load supply 24 V DC; output rated current 0.5 A each, aggregate 4 A per module. At signal 1 permissible current is 7 mA to 0.6 A and output voltage is at least L+ minus 1 V; at signal 0 residual current is at most 0.3 mA. Lamp load maximum 5 W. Switching frequencies: resistive 100 Hz, inductive 2 Hz, lamp 10 Hz. Output parallel operation cannot increase current capability. These conditions are not a scalar suitability verdict.
- Manual page 8 example legend calls item 3 an 8DI module, although its heading and output wiring concern 8DO. Identity and mapping here rely on exact-order page 7 and block diagram page 9, not that inconsistent legend.- No fixed links join the selected field outputs or supply endpoints. Optional AUX1 facilities on other compatible A1 bases remain separate; no AUX/M/PE link is inferred.
- The electronic card does not own the separate base clamps. Internal operating-supply endpoints are functional interfaces, not an enumeration of mating fingers. Backplane communication/contact inventory and installed per-slot base pairing remain unverified. Compatible A1-base auxiliary clamps are not part of this selected 24-01 arrangement. Preserve partial coverage; no PE, shield or AUX bond is inferred.

The compatible TM-E15C24-01 column is on p.7 of the exact manual. Optional AUX1 terminals shown in other columns belong to those different bases. The model includes only the chosen eight-clamp arrangement.

# Weidmüller 1826790000 — bare insert candidate

Exact order **1826790000 = HDC HEE 32 MC**. The [manufacturer data sheet](https://datasheet.weidmueller.com/pdf/en/1826790000/scope/2/), created 27 February 2026, page 1 states explicitly that crimp contacts are **not delivered with the inserts**. Pages 1, 3 and 4 were read; the PE-marked dimensional drawing on page 4 was visually inspected. A normal TLS download is retained locally with selected renders.

The source describes a 32-pole male connector insert. That is its capacity when populated, not proof that 32 contacts belong to this catalog order. The bare body is modeled with one aggregate documented PE interface and no numbered conductive terminals or fixed links. Individual contacts and their wire sizes/plating must be separate selected components. Empty cavity numbers must never create continuity.

Page 3 documents screw PE termination; page 4 shows PE-marked frame interfaces. Their individual clamp/mating/frame bonding matrix is not fully specified, so coverage remains **partial**. Required aggregate PE is an explicit library policy; a presence check does not prove external protective bonding. No housing bond is inferred.

Catalog 500 V / 16 A DIN EN 61984 and 600 V AC/DC UL/CSA capabilities apply with appropriate fitted contacts and derating. No scalar terminal current or nominal circuit voltage is assigned to the empty insert. The PE screw conditions remain in model notes.

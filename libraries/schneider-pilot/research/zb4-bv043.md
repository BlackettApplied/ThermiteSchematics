# zb4-bv043 — Schneider ZB4-BV043

## Identity

- **Manufacturer identity (verified):** Schneider Electric **ZB4BV043** — "Head for
  pilot light, Harmony XB4, metal, red, 22 mm, universal LED, plain lens."
- The manufacturer catalog prints the code **without** the presentation hyphen
  (`ZB4BV043`). The source spelling `ZB4-BV043` differs only by that hyphen; per
  instruction the expected order number `ZB4-BV043` is retained and the hyphen is
  documented as a presentation artifact. Normalized identity matches
  (`schneider electric` / `zb4-bv043`).

## What ZB4BV043 is (and is not)

- It is a **head only**: a red **plain lens in a metal bezel** for a Ø22 cut-out. It is
  the front/operator part of a pilot light with an _integral (universal) LED_.
- The **same red head serves the 24 V, 110–120 V and 230–240 V bodies** (composition
  table). A head therefore carries **no electrical rating and no terminals of its own**.
- The **light source and the screw-clamp supply terminals live in the body**
  (`ZB4BVB4` / `ZB4BVG4` / `ZB4BVM4`), which is a **separately ordered part** and is
  **not** part of this order code. Those LED/supply terminals are deliberately **not**
  imported into the head.
- "Universal LED" in the title names the pilot-light family the head fits, not a light
  source inside the head.

## Electrical vs mechanical scope

- **Electrical scope: none.** Zero terminals, no contact block, no LED/lamp, no
  supply, return, shield, communication or PE terminal on the head.
- **Mechanical scope:** lens + metal bezel, snap-fit onto the pilot-light body; Ø22
  panel mounting. Environmental figures quoted by distributors (IP66 / NEMA 4X-13,
  −25…+70 °C) describe the assembled sealed unit, not a switching/supply rating of a
  bare head, so no `rating` is asserted.
- **Bonding/attachment interface:** the metal bezel's earthing path through the panel
  cut-out and the body is **not documented as an electrical terminal**. No bonding pin
  is asserted; this is modeled as _no terminal_ with **partial** coverage rather than a
  proven bond (per policy: a metal collar/head is not a contact block, and an
  undocumented bond is not a manufacturer fact).

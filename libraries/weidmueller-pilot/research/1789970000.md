These are the positions in the manufacturer's printed view; no as-installed orientation or phase/neutral allocation is inferred. Numbering was read from the female drawing itself.

Each numbered power contact is represented by its cable-side axial endpoint `C.n` and female mating endpoint `M.n`. The `C.`/`M.` prefixes are authoring aliases; the numbers are manufacturer contact identities. The four fixed paths are **C.1–M.1, C.2–M.2, C.3–M.3 and C.4–M.4**. Historical physical **p.153/B.111** supplies the axial assembly section and instructions: the conductor enters the contact from the cable side and clamping is accessed through the corresponding mating-face contact. This supports the separate intrinsic conductive paths, not bridges between contacts. Single-terminal functions and connector metadata do not join nets; the coordinator must materialize the declared fixed links as explicit instance jumpers.

One **aggregate PE endpoint** therefore represents the documented protective-earth interface. It does not assert a central PE pin, exactly one PE screw, a `C.PE–M.PE` link or a housing bond. Connection coverage is **partial** for that physical subdivision. **Required PE is an explicit library policy for modeled protective-earth use**; an authored connection satisfies presence only and does not prove an external earth source or bonding adequacy.

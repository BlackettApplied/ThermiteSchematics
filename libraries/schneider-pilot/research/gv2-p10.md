# gv2-p10 — Schneider GV2-P10

Decision: **candidate**

## Identity

- Verified manufacturer identity: **Schneider Electric GV2P10**, TeSys GV2 /
  TeSys Deca motor circuit breaker. The manufacturer's own datasheet spells the
  reference **`GV2P10`** (no hyphen); the source hyphen in "GV2-P10" is a
  presentation separator. Device short name in the datasheet is `GV2P`.
- The source's "operating with rotary knob" and "thermic-magnetic" map to the
  GV2**P** range (rotary handle, thermal-magnetic), not the pushbutton GV2**ME**.
  The US digest confirms GV2P uses the "rotary handle" column; GV2ME uses the
  "push button" column (Digest 0100CT1501, Table 18.123).
- No successor substitution needed: GV2P10 is a currently commercialised product
  (datasheet Product Life Status: Commercialised).

## Evidence (opened, primary / manufacturer-authored)

1. **Schneider product data sheet — Characteristics GV2P10** (Jul 27, 2023),
   RS distributor mirror of the Schneider-authored sheet.
   Confirms: Motor circuit breaker, TeSys Deca / TeSys GV2, **3P**,
   thermal-magnetic, screw clamp terminals, **rotary handle**;
   thermal adjustment **4…6.3 A** (IEC 60947-4-1); In 6.3 A; **magnetic tripping
   current 78 A** (fixed); Ith 6.3 A; **Ue 690 V AC**, **Ui 690 V AC**,
   Uimp 6 kV; AC-3 / Category A (IEC 60947-2/-4-1); network type AC, 50/60 Hz;
   Icu 100 kA @ ≤500 V, 6 kA @ 690 V; phase-failure sensitive; suitable for
   isolation; power dissipation 2.5 W/pole; tightening torque 1.7 N·m;
   45 × 89 × 97 mm; 35 mm DIN rail or panel mount.
   Motor power: 2.2 kW @ 400/415 V, 3 kW @ 500 V, 4 kW @ 690 V AC.
2. **TeSys GV Catalogue 2017 (EN)** — "Motor circuit breakers GV2, GV3, GV4 and
   GV7", manufacturer mirror. PDF p.9 terminal/scheme graphics
   (DF510744.eps magnetic; **DF569068.eps thermal-magnetic with rotary control =
   GV2P**) **visually inspected**: 3-pole device, line terminals **1/L1, 3/L2,
   5/L3** (top), load terminals **2/T1, 4/T2, 6/T3** (bottom), three poles
   mechanically **ganged** and operated by a single rotary handle. Under-voltage
   trip (D1/D2) and fault-signalling contacts (97/98, 05/06) are drawn as
   **separate add-on modules with their own terminals** — not part of the base
   breaker.
3. **Schneider Digest 0100CT1501, Section 18** (© 2017, 6/20/2017), Schneider
   portal (job-provided shared PDF). PDF p.33 Table 18.123: `GV2P10` selection
   row = **4–6.3 A** thermal setting, rotary handle. GV2 family described as a
   3-pole unit combining a manual disconnect, class-10 ambient-compensated
   thermal overload relay and instantaneous magnetic trip in one body. Auxiliary
   contact blocks (GVAE/GVAN), visible isolation block (GV2AK00) and line-side
   barrier (GV2GH7) are **separate accessory orders**.

## Connection model (base device only)

- **Six main power terminals**, three ganged poles:
  pole1 = 1/L1↔2/T1, pole2 = 3/L2↔4/T2, pole3 = 5/L3↔6/T3.
- Terminal keys use the pole numbers `1..6`; full IEC markings (1/L1 … 6/T3)
  recorded in descriptions.
- **No PE/earth terminal** on this DIN-rail motor circuit breaker (unlike an Ex
  enclosure product); no shield, no communication, no signalling terminal in the
  base. Auxiliary/under-voltage/shunt/signalling modules are separate orders and
  are **not** modelled here.
- Poles are switched contacts; they are modelled as `ganged_with` mechanical
  linkage only. They are **not** permanent net joins — no `fixedLinks`.
- connectionCoverage = **complete** for the bare GV2P10 (its entire physical
  terminal inventory is the six main terminals).

## Ratings modelling

- Per-pole rating recorded as AC with current **6.3 A** = In / conventional
  thermal current Ith, i.e. the thermal adjustment **upper bound** (4–6.3 A is
  field-set), a capability — not an assigned trip setting or wire ampacity.
- `nominal_voltage` deliberately **omitted**: Ue/Ui 690 V AC is a maximum
  capability across a 230–690 V range, not the assigned circuit voltage
  . 690 V capability and the
  conditional Icu/motor-power table are kept in modeling notes.

## Unresolved (do not block candidate)

- Application circuit voltage/current are not assigned and are left out of scalar ratings.
- Whether an installed assembly carries an add-on auxiliary/signal block
  in-panel (those would be separate orders with their own terminals).
- Screw-clamp conductor cross-section range not enumerated (torque 1.7 N·m only).

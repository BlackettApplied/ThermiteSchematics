# 1sca022710r0100 — ABB 1SCA022710R0100 (OT250E03P)

## Identity (established)

- Order number **1SCA022710R0100** = extended product type **OT250E03P**.
- ABB **switch-disconnector**, 3-pole, front operated, base mounting, pistol
  (ON/OFF) handle with shaft and terminal-bolt kit; IEC 60947-3.
- Confirmed in an opened ABB selection table: catalogue 1SCC301020C0201 p.24,
  row `3 | 250 | 250/250 | OT250E03P | 1SCA022710R0100`, and in the ABB product
  data sheet (Product ID 1SCA022710R0100, EAN 6417019225944).

This is a bare **switch-disconnector** (isolator), not a fuse, fuse base, or
switch-fuse assembly (OS/OSM), and not a motorised/OTM type.

## Ratings (manufacturer, conditional — see notes, do not flatten)

From ABB product data sheet 1SCC301020C0201 and catalogue technical data:

- Number of poles: 3. Standard: IEC 60947-3. Pollution degree 3.
- Rated operational current Ie = **250 A** for AC-21A (380–1000 V), AC-22A and
  AC-23A (380–690 V). Conventional thermal current Ith/Ithe (enclosed) = 250 A @ 40 °C.
- Rated operational power AC-23A (Pe): 75 kW @220–240 V, 145 kW @400–415 V,
  170 kW @500 V, 250 kW @690 V.
- Rated insulation voltage Ui = **1000 V** (IEC/EN 60664-1); main-circuit rated
  operational voltage 1000 V; rated impulse Uimp = 12 kV.
- Icw = 8 kA for 1 s; Icm = 30 kA @690 V. Power loss ≈ 6.5 W/pole at rated current.
- Terminals: **lug terminals**, 20 mm wide, M8 bolt, tightening 15…22 N·m
  (IEC 60947-1). Position of line terminals is reversible: "Top In–Bottom Out"
  or "Bottom In–Top Out". Mechanical durability 20 000 operations.
- "Special Functions: No" — no auxiliary contacts, motor, or release in this order.

Ratings are AC utilization categories; DC ratings are not covered by the reviewed
documents. Ui 1000 V and the 250/145 kW figures are capabilities, not an assigned
circuit voltage/power.

## Connection topology (established) and terminal marking (finding)

- The device has **6 main terminals** = 3 poles × (line side + load side). A single
  pistol handle opens/closes all three poles **simultaneously** (ganged); each pole
  is an independently isolated switched path.
- **No PE/earth terminal, no neutral**: this is the 3-pole variant (E03). A neutral
  link (OS_N3/OXN250) and a 4th pole (OTZ250E) are _separate accessories_; the 4-pole
  variant is a different order code (OT250E04P, 1SCA022710R0520). Auxiliary contacts
  (OA1G10/OA3G01) and interlocks are also separately ordered and are **not** part of
  1SCA022710R0100.
- **Terminal markings remain unverified.** Manufacturer terminal markings were not established in the reviewed installation and dimension drawings. L1/L2/L3 and T1/T2/T3 are explicit functional authoring aliases for the two ends of each independent pole, not claimed inscriptions or an as-built phase assignment. The product data sheet permits Top In–Bottom Out or Bottom In–Top Out; these aliases do not require a fixed line/load direction.

## Continuity / fixed links

None. A switch-disconnector must never be modelled as permanently joined merely
because a closed pole conducts. Each pole is a switched contact; there are no
factory jumpers, buses, or internal permanent links in the 3-pole base device.

## Decision

**candidate.** Identity, 3-pole switched topology, ganged operation, absence of
PE/neutral/aux in this order, and ratings are established from opened ABB sources.
The six-position main-terminal inventory and three ganged switched paths are supported. Coverage remains partial for physical inscriptions and detailed terminal/accessory interfaces; functional aliases do not establish those missing details.

## Unresolved / conditional

- Physical terminal inscriptions have not been established; `L1/L2/L3`, `T1/T2/T3` are functional aliases and line/load orientation is reversible.
- DC utilization ratings not covered by reviewed AC-category documents.
- Optional accessories (aux contacts OA_, neutral link, 4th pole OTZ250E, interlocks)
  are separate order codes and are not modelled here.

## Independent review

The coordinator visually reviewed installation pages5 and8 against the candidate. Absence of readable labels in a drawing does not prove that the manufactured device is unmarked. The reviewed candidate therefore removes that initial overclaim and retains partial marking/interface coverage. No invented IEC numbering was substituted.

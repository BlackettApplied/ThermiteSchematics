# gv2-p07 — Schneider Electric GV2-P07

## Decision

`candidate`. Exact catalog identity and the base connection inventory are
established from Schneider-authored product data and the manufacturer's TeSys GV
manual-starter schematic.

## Identity and ratings — primary evidence

Three Schneider-authored documents agree exactly with the source:

1. **Square D / Schneider spec sheet `SQD-GV2P07.PDF`** (iportal.se.com, Dec 06 2017):
   Range TeSys GV2; device short name **GV2P**; product type **circuit breaker**,
   device application **Motor**; trip technology **Thermal-magnetic**; **3P**;
   **Control type: "Rotary knob"** (verbatim match to source); [In] rated /
   trip-unit rating **1.6…2.5 A**; magnetic tripping current **33.5 A**;
   [Ue] **690 V AC 50/60 Hz**; [Ui] 690 V; Icu 100 kA @230/400/440/500 V, 8 kA @690 V;
   **Connections – terminals: screw clamp, 2 conductors 1…6 mm² solid / 1.5…6 mm²
   flexible / 1…4 mm² with ferrule**, tightening **1.7 N·m**; phase-failure
   sensitivity **Yes**; suitability for isolation **Yes**; 89×45×97 mm; motor
   power 0.75 kW@400 V … 1.5 kW@690 V.
2. **Schneider IE product datasheet `GV2P07_DATASHEET`** (RS mirror, Jul 27 2023):
   corroborates "Motor circuit breaker, TeSys Deca, 3P, 1.6 to 2.5 A, thermal
   magnetic, screw clamp terminals, **rotary handle**"; In 2.5 A; magnetic 33.5 A;
   Ue 690 V AC; DIN-rail 35 mm / panel (2×M4); durability 100 000 cycles.
3. **Schneider US Digest `0100CT1501` Section 18** (iportal.se.com): selection
   Table 18.123 (PDF p.33) lists adjustment **1.6–2.5 A → GV2ME07 / GV2P07**, with
   the **GV2P column headed "rotary handle"** (GV2ME = push button in this US
   catalog). Auxiliary-contact blocks (`GVAE1`, `GVAE11`, `GVAN11`, `GVAE20`,
   `GVAN20`) and voltage releases are listed as **separate add-on orders**
   (Table 18.134); GV2P UL 508 Type E use requires line-side barrier `GV2GH7`.

Note on naming: US catalog maps GV2P→rotary, GV2ME→push button; the IE datasheet
also states GV2P07 = rotary handle. Both agree the ordered part `GV2P07` is the
rotary-operated device, consistent with the source "rotary knob".

## Connection model

- **6 main power terminals in 3 poles**: line 1/L1, 3/L2, 5/L3; load 2/T1, 4/T2,
  6/T3. The model preserves the complete dual markings as terminal keys.
- **3 poles mechanically linked** — single rotary operator, common thermal-magnetic
  trip. Modeled as `ganged_with` internal relations. Poles are switching contacts,
  **not** permanent net joins → **no fixed links**.
- **No auxiliary contacts, releases or signaling** in the GV2P07 base order (those
  are separate `GVA*` / `GV2AK00` orders). **No protective-earth terminal**
  (IP20 moulded body); panel/enclosure bonding is external and not a device pin.
- Coverage = **complete** for the ordered base device: the 6 main terminals are its
  entire electrical interface.

## Ratings modeling

- Device is multi-voltage (rated up to Ue 690 V AC; usable 230–690 V). Per modeling
  guidance, `rating.nominal_voltage` is **left unassigned** on terminals (a range
  maximum is a capability, not an assigned supply voltage); 690 V AC max is recorded
  here and in `modelingNotes`. Terminals carry `voltage_type: AC` (device is AC / AC-3).
- Thermal current is an **adjustment range 1.6–2.5 A**; the scalar `current: 2.5`
  on terminals is the upper adjustment bound (capability), not a trip setting or
  conductor ampacity. Fixed magnetic release ≈ 33.5 A (~13×In upper).

## Unresolved / cautions

An exact catalog match does not establish as-built wiring.

# R. STAHL 8575/12-200

Coordinator follow-up replaced inferred numeric aliases with verified **L1/L2/L3** contact markings.

The manufacturer-authored [2010 CES catalogue](https://media.w-f.ch/asset_external/downloads/lieferanten/r.stahl%20ag/datenbl%C3%A4tter/steckvorrichtung_2.pdf), revision **2010-06·EK00·III·de**, E3/2, was read and visually inspected, including an enlarged view of plug diagram **09193E00**. The exact selection identifies a three-pole, 16 A, 20–25 V AC, 50/60 Hz violet plug. The drawing labels L1, L2 and L3. There is no dedicated PE or neutral contact. E3/9 gives family technical data. No other order supplies its pinout.

Each passive plug contact has a cable-side endpoint and a mating endpoint, represented by C.L1/M.L1 through C.L3/M.L3. C/M are authoring aliases, not additional physical contacts. Three explicit fixed links represent factory continuity; neither bus functions nor connector metadata creates nets. Terminal use is application-dependent.

Coverage remains partial: individual cable-clamp legends, installed orientation and phase use are unverified. The 20–25 V operating range remains documentation; no range maximum is encoded as the compiler's exact nominal-voltage constraint. Rated current is 16 A. Mechanical keying and manufacturer contact labels do not assert installed mating compatibility or protection suitability.

# 8575-13-200 - R. STAHL 8575/13-200

## Decision and exact identity

**Candidate, with partial connection coverage.** The manufacturer catalogue
**2010-06-EK00-III-de, E3/2** directly lists **8575/13-200** as the CES 16 A
extra-low-voltage wall socket: **3P, 20-25 V AC, 50/60 Hz, violet, no auxiliary
coding nose**. This resolves the original research's missing exact-order source. Its word "plug"
does not change the ordered 8575/13 socket into a plug. No successor or different
suffix is substituted.

## Supplemental seed and document applicability

The supplied `references/stahl-8575-manual-2007-seed.pdf` is R. STAHL document
8575618300, **S-BA-8575/13/14-01-de-11/04/2007**. Its source URL was not established
independently; it is supplementary local evidence rather than a replacement for
S1's cited 05.01 revision. The seed.

Visually reviewed printed p.5 / physical p.7 gives ELV 3P figure **06199E00**;
printed p.6 / physical p.8 explicitly excludes PE; printed p.9 / physical p.11
identifies the 8575/1.-200 flange as three-pole. These agree with the modeled
contact inventory and interlock. The original references to Z0099 and 06196E00
were transcription errors; the correct figures are **Z0299** and **06199E00**.

The revisions do **not** agree on all ratings. The 2007 manual printed p.4 /
physical p.6 states **IP66 / IP55 mated**, whereas S1 printed p.3 and S2 E3/9 state
**IP54**. The 2007 table also does not give S1's >=100 Hz current derating.
Documented ratings are therefore revision-qualified; installed hardware and
nameplate suitability are not resolved by this model.

## Electrical model

- Cable-side functional endpoints: `C.L1`, `C.L2`, `C.L3`.
- Mating contacts: `M.L1`, `M.L2`, `M.L3`.
- `C` and `M` are authoring side aliases. L1/L2/L3 are manufacturer contact
  identities, but exact cable-clamp stamps and screw positions are unverified.
  No numeric pin renaming, rear-view coordinates or installed phase assignment
  is asserted. The base nose is at 6h; this variant has no auxiliary nose.
- Three ganged switched-pole functions use the open OFF drawing reference.
  This reference is not a claim about an unactuated mechanism or a simulated
  state. **No fixed links** cross these switched paths. The model has **no PE**.
- Terminal ratings retain **AC / 16 A**, within the exact 20-25 V / 50-60 Hz
  coded scope. **No `nominal_voltage` scalar is assigned.** The family maximum
  of 50 V is a capability; the engine compares nominal values exactly and would
  otherwise reject a valid 24 V application with E302. An arbitrary scalar
  within the coded voltage range is not inferred either.
- Individual contacts are application-dependent, so `requiredTerminalKeys` is
  empty. This does not establish installation completeness.
- Coverage remains **partial**. Optional supporting-terminal block 151307 and
  other optional equipment are outside this base model; installed presence is
  unverified. No accessory pins or bonding screw are invented.

These four insertion parts are represented by two shared terminal keys, preserving ABB's terminal numbering. Supply direction is selectable. Conductor capacity at either end: 35 mm² solid/stranded or 25 mm² flexible; busbar 10 mm²; torque 2.8 Nm.

The bare order has one interruptible power path and no neutral, PE/bond, shield, signal, communication or supplied auxiliary terminals. Catalog accessory compatibility does not mean accessories are included. Connection coverage is complete for this bare identity.

Factory-continuity specification: **no fixed links between distinct terminal keys**. The pole can open; its contact metadata must never join nets.

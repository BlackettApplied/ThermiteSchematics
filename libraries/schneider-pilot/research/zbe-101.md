# zbe-101 — Schneider ZBE-101 / ZBE101

Decision: **candidate**. Exact evidence establishes a bare single standard normally open contact block with **two screw-clamp terminals, 3 and 4**. Both terminals are passive and application-dependent. There is one normally open contact function, no fixed link, and complete connection coverage for this bare block.

## Exact diagram and identity

**S1 — [Direct manufacturer ZBE101 product PDF](https://www.se.com/au/en/product/download-pdf/ZBE101), generated 12 September 2026, nine pages.** Retrieved with normal TLS and the research User-Agent. Pages 1 and 6–9 were independently rendered and visually inspected. Page 8 shows the block's actual **ZBE-101** marking alongside the normally open contact diagram ending at `.3` and `.4`. Pages 6 and 9 show the field face with **two distinct screw clamps labeled 3 and 4**, separated by the green mechanical plunger. The other views distinguish clips and plunger from electrical endpoints.

The printed dots on the side diagram do not create extra terminals. The actual field keys are `3` and `4`; no `13` / `14` alias or presumed IEC-style tens digit is added. The exact manufacturer's body artwork supplies direct evidence that the source's hyphenated **ZBE-101** is the product whose catalogue reference is **ZBE101**.

**S2 — [Manufacturer-authored ZBE101 datasheet at RS](https://docs.rs-online.com/2ef4/0900766b816e9638.pdf), 9 May 2023, three pages.** Pages 1–2 were independently visually inspected. The page 1 photograph already carries the same ZBE-101 marking and NO 3/4 diagram, corroborating S1 across document dates. Its text identifies one single standard slow-break NO contact, silver alloy Ag/Ni material, screw clamps and **without positive opening**. The exact 2023 evidence is retained; current artwork is not treated as proof of a specific installed manufacturing revision.

**S3 — [Manufacturer Harmony XB4 catalogue](https://iportal.se.com/Contents/docs/HARMONY%20XB4%20METAL%20CONTROL%20AND%20SIGNALING%20UNITS_CATALOG.PDF), October 2021, V13.0, DIA5ED2121212EN.** Physical page 78 / printed 76 was independently visually inspected. The exact ZBE101 row identifies a standard single NO contact block. The page separately lists **ZB4BZ009 fixing collar** and **ZB4BZ101 collar-inclusive single-NO assembly**. Specific low-power, early-make, monitoring and high-power versions have other references; their properties are not transferred.

## Inventory and model boundary

| Item                    | Model treatment                                                           |
| ----------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Screw 3                 | Passive control terminal 3; not universally required                      |
| Screw 4                 | Passive control terminal 4; not universally required                      |
| Contact                 | One `contact` function, normal state `open`, existing `contact-no` symbol |
| Plunger and clips       | Mechanical operating/attachment interfaces, no electrical terminal        |
| Head, collar, enclosure | Separate assembly choices, not included in this bare-block order          |

The two contact terminals remain distinct physical nets. Function membership never shorts 3 to 4, even though the contact can close during operation. No coil, lamp, supply/return, PE, shield, communication port or extra contact is part of this block's documented inventory. Mechanical attachment to separate mounting hardware does not establish a protective bond. Coverage is complete **for the bare block**, without claiming coverage or bonding of its containing assembly.

The block does not determine whether its eventual actuator is a pushbutton, selector or emergency-stop head. The model therefore uses the ordinary NO contact symbol and does not assert an actuator-specific or safety monitoring behavior. Passive terminal use remains application-dependent.

## Conditional ratings

No scalar terminal voltage or current rating is assigned. Both exact sheets give the following operational-current conditions per EN/IEC 60947-5-1:

- AC-15 / A600: 6 A at 120 V; 3 A at 240 V; 1.2 A at 600 V.
- DC-13 / Q600: 0.55 A at 125 V; 0.27 A at 250 V; 0.1 A at 600 V.

The separate **10 A free-air thermal current**, **600 V insulation at pollution degree 3**, **6 kV impulse withstand**, and **10 A gG short-circuit protection** must not be flattened into an unconditional 10 A / 600 V switching capability. These are not supply-voltage requirements.

Screw-clamp conditions are at least 1 × 0.22 mm² without cable end and at most 2 × 1.5 mm² with cable end, 0.8–1.2 N·m tightening torque. Multiple conductors accepted under a screw do not create duplicate terminal keys. IP20 and −40…70 °C ambient conditions remain documentary. Actual duty and assembly suitability are project-level checks.

# gv2-p22 — Schneider GV2-P22 research

## Identity

- **Verified manufacturer identity:** Schneider Electric **GV2P22**, TeSys GV2 /
  TeSys Deca **motor circuit breaker** (motor-protection circuit breaker / manual
  starter-protector), 3-pole, thermal-magnetic, **rotary-handle (knob) control**,
  screw-clamp terminals. The manufacturer catalog spelling is `GV2P22` (no
  hyphen); the source/job `GV2-P22` hyphen is a presentation separator, not a
  different reference. No successor substitution was made — GV2P22 is still a
  commercialised catalog reference.

## Terminal inventory (base unit)

Six main power terminals only, per the inspected schematic:

- Line (top): `1/L1`, `3/L2`, `5/L3`
- Load (bottom): `2/T1`, `4/T2`, `6/T3`

Three switched poles: `1/L1–2/T1`, `3/L2–4/T2`, `5/L3–6/T3`. The single rotary
handle and common trip operate all three poles together (mechanical ganging).
No protective-earth/bond terminal exists on the base device (IP20, DIN-rail
clip or 2×M4 panel mount) — the schematic shows none, so none is invented.

Connection coverage = **complete** for the ordered base unit: all six main
terminals are documented and the base breaker has no other electrical
connections. Auxiliary/signalling contacts (GVAE11/GVAE20, GVAED…), shunt/
undervoltage releases (D1/D2), and short-circuit/position signalling (97/98,
05/06) are **separate accessory orders** shown in the same catalog page — they
are NOT part of GV2P22 and are excluded.

## Modeling decisions

- Three poles modeled as `contact` functions (circuitSymbol `breaker`), ganged
  via `internal_relations: ganged_with`. Poles are **switched contacts** and do
  **not** create permanent net joins → `conformance.fixedLinks = []`. Breaker
  poles are not modeled as fixed links (per job hints).
- Terminal keys preserve the verified dual markings `n/Lx` and `n/Tx`.
- No `required: true` terminals: main power terminals are application-dependent
  (which poles are landed depends on the circuit); there is no universally
  required PE on this device → `requiredTerminalKeys = []`.
- Ratings: `voltage_type: AC`, `current: 25` (In = max thermal setting) placed on
  each pole. Full voltage/
  breaking-capacity capability is recorded here and in modelingNotes rather than
  flattened into a misleading scalar.- renderKind = `circuit`.

## Unresolved / cautions

- Thermal trip setting as installed (dial position within 20–25 A) is an
  installation parameter, not verified.
- Magnetic trip 327 A is fixed (non-adjustable); trip-curve/backup-fuse
  coordination and any Type E/F combination barriers (GV2GH7) are not evaluated.
- Identity/terminal match does not by itself establish full as-built connection
  coverage, engineering suitability or source consistency (per job matchCaution).

## Paths and aliases

Manufacturer field markings `1`–`8` are retained; front-view rows are `1/5`, `2/6`, `3/7`, `4/8`. Each field position has its own path to the electronic-module interface, represented by an explicit matching link `n–EM.n`. `EM.n` is a base-side library alias, not a verified connector cavity number. No field positions are bridged by this base model.

The reviewed follow-up replaces schematic-position names `PWR.UPPER/LOWER` with documented manufacturer bus names `P1/P2`. Each has independent `L`, `R` and `EM` interface aliases: left-to-right continuity plus the electronic-module tap. Four explicit jumpers represent the two separate three-endpoint networks. These names do not assign polarity, voltage or physical connector coordinates.

`AUX1.L–AUX1.R` is the thirteenth fixed link, independently passing through the base. No field-to-AUX1 or AUX1-to-EM path is drawn on this variant. Field terminals 4/8 remain ordinary EM paths; no A3/A4/A7/A8 clamps are invented. AUX1 is configurable and is not assigned permanently to PE, P2 or FG. The system manual cautions that separate AUX1 and P1/P2 drawing paths do not establish safety isolation when different potentials are used.

Functions and metadata create no net continuity. All 13 proposed fixed links require explicit project jumpers. The model asserts no conduction inside a plugged-in electronic card. Required-terminal inventory remains empty because field use depends on that separately selected card.

## Partial scope

The manufacturer also documents an EM-to-DIN-rail noise-diversion spring contact (FG); its mating contact identity is unresolved and this candidate omits it explicitly. No backplane or FG jumper is fabricated. Optional shielding hardware, compatibility, installed revision, conductor capacity and actual loading remain separate application checks. No `nominal_voltage`, unconditional current rating, or universal 24 V/M assignment is authored.

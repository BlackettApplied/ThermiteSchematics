# Releco C4-A40X/230VAC — c4-a40x-230vac

**Decision: candidate.** Exact order identity and the terminal/coil diagram were
established from primary manufacturer evidence.

## Identity

- Order number: **C4-A40X/230VAC** (source), catalog form **C4-A4xX/AC…V →
  C4-A40X/AC230V** — a punctuation/ordering equivalence for the same coil
  voltage (230 V AC), not a substitution.
- Found in **ComatReleco "World of Relays" (WoR) 3.2**, section _1.3 Industrial
  Relays – pluggable_, family **C4-A4x**, physical PDF page **55**.
- Code decode (from the same page):
  - `C4` = 4-pole changeover relay (4 CO), **Faston.110** tabs.
  - contact-material digit `0` → **AgNi** (`C4-A40`; `8`=AgNi+5µAu).
  - suffix `X` = **LED** coil-status indicator.
  - `/230VAC` = **230 V AC coil** (50 Hz standard; 60 Hz also offered).
- Brand: **ComatReleco**; "Releco" is retained as the historical brand per the
  job identity (`expectedManufacturer: "Releco"`).

## Source-vs-manufacturer note

The source BOM labels the item _"Contactor 4-pole 230VAC / MR-C serie / four pole
relay"_. The manufacturer documents a 4-pole **plug-in industrial relay** (series
**C4**), not a contactor; the historical **"MR-C serie"** wording is retained without claiming that
it is a current catalog designation. The order code itself
(C4-A40X/230VAC) is exact and matches the catalog family unambiguously.

## Terminal / wiring diagram (Fig. 1, p.55 — inspected visually)

Four changeover contacts, each printed with the **physical Faston pin number**
(bold 1–14) and the **IEC contact alias**:

| Pole | COM (physical / IEC) | NC (physical / IEC) | NO (physical / IEC) |
| ---- | -------------------- | ------------------- | ------------------- |
| 1    | 3 / 11               | 1 / 12              | 2 / 14              |
| 2    | 6 / 21               | 4 / 22              | 5 / 24              |
| 3    | 9 / 31               | 7 / 32              | 8 / 34              |
| 4    | 12 / 41              | 10 / 42             | 11 / 44             |

Coil: **13 = A1(+)**, **14 = A2**. Terminals are keyed here by the physical
1–14 tab numbers; the IEC 11/12/14… aliases are printed in the _same_
manufacturer diagram (explicit proof, not inferred). Fig. 4 (dimensions) confirms
the physical tab layout `1,4,7,10 / 2,5,8,11 / 3,6,9,12 / 13,14`.

## Ratings (p.55)

- **Contacts (AgNi):** rated current **10 A**. Conditional switching maxima:
  **10 A / 250 V AC (AC-1)** and **10 A / 30 V DC (DC-1)**; rated load AC
  2500 VA; inrush 30 A / 20 ms; recommended min. load 10 mA / 5 V. The contacts
  are rated for **both AC and DC**, so no scalar switching voltage or AC/DC type
  is assigned to the contact terminals — no unconditional current scalar is placed on
  them; 250 V is a capability maximum, not an assigned circuit voltage.
- **Coil:** 230 V AC (50 Hz; 60 Hz also available). Operating range 0.8–1.1 UN;
  pick-up ≤ 0.8 UN; release ≥ 0.1 UN; consumption **2.4 VA / 1.4 W**; the 230 V AC
  coil table row gives **6850 Ω, 10.4 mA**. `A1 (+)` is a DC polarity marker and
  does not apply to this AC coil.
- Insulation: 1 kV open contact, 2.5 kV contact/contact and contact/coil;
  overvoltage cat. III; pollution degree 3. Standards **IEC/EN 60947; IEC/EN
  61810**; approvals apply to C4-A40.

## Suppression / LED

Suffix `X` = **LED indication with rectification at the coil** (WoR3.2 p.11,
additionally inspected by the coordinator); it adds no terminal and creates no link
between contacts. This AC `X` variant has **no free-wheeling diode** (that is the
DC `DX`/`FX` variants) and **no RC suppressor** (`R` variant). No fixed internal
links are modeled.

## Scope / accessories (separate parts — not modeled here)

Socket **S4-J / S4-P**, blanking plug **SO-NP**, wall-mount adapter **S5-R**, test
button **S5-HP** are separately ordered accessories. This type models the **bare
relay's 14 Faston tabs only**; socket cage/clamp terminals are out of scope. The
bare relay has no PE, neutral, shield or communication terminal, so terminal
coverage of the relay body is complete.

## Modeling summary

- 14 terminals: 12 contact tabs (4 CO) + 2 coil tabs.
- Each pole = two `contact` functions (NC closed, NO open) sharing the COM tab;
  one `coil` function. `coil` (A1/A2 = pins 13/14) is the universally **required**
  connection (library policy); contact positions are application-dependent.
- Render: `thermite:relay` device symbol; per-function circuit marks
  `contact-nc` / `contact-no` / `coil`. `renderKind: circuit`.
- No `fixedLinks` (coil/LED do not join contacts; poles are independent).

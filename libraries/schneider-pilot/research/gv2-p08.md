## Connection and modeling specification

Complete inventory for the bare order: **1/L1, 3/L2, 5/L3, 2/T1, 4/T2, 6/T3**. Markings are retained verbatim, without functional aliases. No separate neutral, PE, shield, supply, communication, auxiliary or release connections are documented for this order. Add-on electrical blocks are separate components, not implicit installed contacts.

Three contact functions use the existing `breaker` circuit symbol and `ganged_with` relations. Open state means the depicted OFF/open reference state; it does not assert the installation's actual switch position. Each pole represents its complete protected switched path. Thermal and magnetic elements have no separately accessible terminals; protection curves are not simulated. **No permanent fixed links**: even ON-state conduction is switch-dependent. No terminal is universally required in this reusable passive-device model; actual usage and motor wiring remain application-dependent.

## Ratings and limits

S1: 50/60 Hz AC; Ue and Ui 690 V; thermal range 2.5–4 A; free-air thermal current 4 A; magnetic release 51 A. IEC 60947-2 Icu is 100 kA at 230/240, 400/415, 440 and 500 V AC, but 8 kA at 690 V AC. These conditional capabilities remain notes; no assigned `nominal_voltage` or scalar interrupt rating is authored. No installation trip setting is inferred. S1 lists 1.7 N·m screw torque and conditional conductor capacities; wiring selection is not automated.

No source-description disagreement in function, adjustment range or operation. Newer S2 uses TeSys Deca branding and a changed product appearance; the older exact S1 and S3 establish historical identity. The proposal does not claim historical appearance equivalence.

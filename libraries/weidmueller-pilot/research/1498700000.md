## Authored topology and limits

C.n and M.n are library aliases for the screw termination and male interface of one numbered passive power contact. Six explicit same-contact fixed links are proposed; singleton functions and connector metadata do not establish continuity. No links join different poles.

PE is an aggregate interface because the reviewed sources do not fully identify every physical PE clamp/mating feature or internal bond. The unsupported central PE pin and split C.PE/M.PE link are removed. `PE.required=true` is explicit library policy for authored connection presence, not proof of external earthing or housing continuity. Unused power contacts are optional. The insert model therefore remains partial.

The historical numbered drawing and current data agree on identity and visible numbering, but installed revision remains unverified. Mating hardware, hood/housing bonding and environmental suitability are outside this insert model.

The current exact datasheet p.3 states IEC 35 A/400 V and UL/CSA 600 V AC/DC. Its approval-specific current rows were read visually and preserved:

No terminal `nominal_voltage`, AC-only assignment or unconditional current scalar is authored. The 8 A entries are not silently changed to 35 A; approval-specific use that depends on different values needs manufacturer clarification.

P.4 distinguishes main-contact screw M4 (0.5–6 mm²/AWG 20–10, 11 mm strip, 1.2–1.5 Nm) from PE screw M5 (rated 6 mm²/AWG 20–10, 10 mm strip, 2–2.5 Nm). The [manufacturer torque sheet](https://assets.dam.weidmueller.com/assets/api/719442ee-190c-4c27-8b06-0fb4d3eaf6d9/original/HDC_Tightening_torques.pdf), 2014/2015 printed A.17, independently corroborates those two HSB torque rows, not PE topology.

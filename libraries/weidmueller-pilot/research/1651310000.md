## Modeling boundary

The supported numbered inventory permits **C.1/M.1, C.3/M.3, C.5/M.5, C.7/M.7 and C.9/M.9**, with one aggregate **PE** endpoint: 11 authoring endpoints total. `C.` means the cable screw termination and `M.` the mating interface of the same numbered contact; these prefixes are library aliases, not manufacturer inscriptions.

The five within-insert fixed conductive paths are **C.n–M.n for n = 1, 3, 5, 7, 9**. These represent the cable-to-mating ends of each passive copper-alloy contact. They must not short different poles together or assert a connection across the external detachable mating interface. Calling 1 and 5 “switch contacts” describes their lagging engagement in a connector pair; it does not establish a normally-open internal relay or a safety-rated interruption function. Do not join the two pilot contacts internally.

Use power roles for 3/7/9 and signal roles for 1/5; ordinary contacts may be unused depending on the project. No capability rating should become a terminal `nominal_voltage`.

An aggregate PE endpoint is appropriate with **partial connection coverage**. S1 p. 3 verifies a screw PE connection and S2/S3 show PE, but the reviewed material does not enumerate and distinguish every physical PE cable-clamp, mating-contact, frame and housing interface. Do not invent `C.PE`/`M.PE`, central PE pin numbering, side-to-side PE links, or housing bonds. **PE required=true is the explicit library policy for modeled protective-earth use**, not a universal manufacturer installation quotation. Connection presence does not prove an upstream bond; external protective bonding must be established by the project. Projects may document deliberate non-use.

## Rejected source interpretation

**S4** is the customer PDF linked directly from this exact product's manufacturer downloads page (S6), filename `1651310000_HDC_HVE_3+2_MS_STP_Blatt__1.pdf`. Its title and CAT.NO. do match the requested order. However, its top view labels **ten body positions 1–10**, and its rating annotation says **630 V / 16 A**. The title-block dates are drawn 24 October 2005 and checked 31 October 2005. This cannot serve alone as a five-populated-contact inventory or as the current rating authority. The distinction between numbered body positions and actual installed contacts matters. Use the exact five-position S3 arrangement and S2 role diagram; do not instantiate all ten positions from S4 or assign its ratings to this model.

S3's introductory English phrase “lagging earth contacts” conflicts with S1's two lagging contacts, the distinct PE symbol and the three-power/two-signal inventory. Do not interpret it as delayed protective-earth operation. The newer explicit contact inventory governs.

This follow-up adds that evidence, replaces its functional aliases with 1/3/5/7/9, and replaces its contradictory split C.PE/M.PE plus PE link with one aggregate PE. Source-only unknowns are preserved rather than turned into invented contacts or limits. No physical mating-half, field jumper, or housing bond is inferred.

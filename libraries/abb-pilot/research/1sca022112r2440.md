# ABB 1SCA022112R2440 — reviewed candidate

Exact order **1SCA022112R2440 = OFAX 00 S2L**, two independent poles, is in the [ABB BaseLine catalog](https://library.e.abb.com/public/12419534cc9c4dedb8baac925c205f26/1SCC316001C0201.pdf), 1SCC316001C0201 / OFA1GB02_09, p.5. The exact row states 160 A, Ui1000 V and 20 mm tabs. The two-pole dimensional arrangement appears on p.7 (S00317A). Construction drawing S00288A on p.3 was visually inspected.

Each pole has two opposed end assemblies. Each assembly includes a main tab, fuse mating contact and built-in2.5 mm² measuring tap. Thus the bounded functional model has **12 endpoints and8 local fixed links**, forming **four isolated nets**. No fuse-gap or inter-pole bridge is present. The removable fuse and optional cable-clamp/bolt accessories are separate parts.

The initial worker omitted the measuring taps and did not distinguish main tab from fuse mating contact. Independent review recovered these from the exact applicable construction and two-pole diagrams. Original files are retained; this candidate implements the more complete inventory. The existing single-pole library file supplied JSON structure only.

P1/P2, TAB/FUSE/TAP and A/B are authoring aliases, not stamped numbers or installation orientation. Coverage remains partial for terminal inscriptions, individual clamp entries and exhaustive mounting interfaces. No PE or DIN-rail bonding claim is made. The160 A base capability is not assigned to measuring taps. Ui1000 V is distinct from the older catalog’s690 V entry; neither becomes a nominal circuit supply.

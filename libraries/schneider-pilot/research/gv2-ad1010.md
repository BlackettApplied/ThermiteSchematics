# gv2-ad1010 — Schneider GV2-AD1010

Decision: **candidate**. Preserve type ID `schneider-pilot:gv2-ad1010`, manufacturer `Schneider Electric`, and order `GV2-AD1010`. The historical manufacturer spells the order **GV2AD1010**; the source hyphen is a presentation separator. No successor substitution is needed. The historical description agrees: left-side block, one NO fault contact plus one NO instantaneous auxiliary contact.

Evidence: [S1, Schneider/Square D, Manual Motor Starters, Controllers, and Protectors, 2520CT0001, 05/01](https://www.fichier-pdf.fr/2018/12/04/45manstart/45manstart.pdf), manufacturer-authored historical document at a public document mirror. Saved as `references/historical-2001.pdf`. Printed and physical PDF pages coincide: 11 exact selection row and mounting illustration; 26 ratings; 27 operation; 58 exact order wiring diagram. All four pages inspected visually; page 58's exact GV2AD1010 cell additionally enlarged to 400 DPI (saved crop with metadata).

Connection inventory: 53 and 54 are the instantaneous NO contact endpoints; 97 and 98 are the NO fault endpoints. Four screw-clamp connections; no supply, return, PE, shield or communication connection belongs to this block. Contacts are application-dependent, so none is universally required. No factory fixed links exist between these endpoints: switching is not permanent continuity. Both functions use `contact-no`; no ganged relation is asserted because position and trip indications differ. Normal means breaker off/reset, without a fault indication. The model does not simulate trip/reset timing.

Ratings from S1 p26 are conditional, not assigned circuit voltage: instantaneous contact IEC Ith 6 A, AC-15 at 100,000 operations 3.3 A at 230–240 V; fault contact IEC Ith 2.5 A, AC-14 at 1,000 operations 0.3 A at 230–240 V. DC-13 at 24 V: 6 A instantaneous/100,000 operations versus 1 A fault/1,000 operations. Other voltages have different limits; see the saved table. Both support AC and DC. No scalar terminal rating is modeled.

Documentation caveats: S1 p27 explicitly includes short-circuit, overload and undervoltage tripping for GV2AD fault indication. Its instantaneous timing chart has apparent normal-state/label inconsistencies; use the exact p58 diagram and p11 composition for the two NO states, without importing that chart's transition timing. [S2, Schneider FAQ FA23920](https://www.se.com/uk/en/faqs/FA23920/) (page displays last modified `2/11/2026`, accessed 2026-09-11) describes the later **GVAD1010** using the same terminal pairs, but mentions magnetic/thermal faults only. It is contextual comparison, not evidence to remove historical undervoltage behavior or declare interchangeability. Exact reset timing and successor equivalence remain unmodeled.

Search record: the supplied [2017 Section 18 catalog](https://iportal.se.com/Contents/docs/0100CT1501_SEC-18.PDF), p18-36, uses GVAD1010, so it was insufficient alone. Manuals.plus exposed text for the 2001 document but its direct download returned HTTP 403; web screenshots did not yield inspectable images. The public Fichier-PDF mirror then supplied the inspectable original. The generic Schneider download reference 2520CT0001 returned 404. An opened October 2002 catalog at Electrol Supply uses revised codes; it was not used to assign this order's terminals. Complete documents remain in this staging directory only. No project, library, build or bespoke verifier was created.

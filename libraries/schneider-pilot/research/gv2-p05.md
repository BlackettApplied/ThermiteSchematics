# gv2-p05 — Schneider Electric GV2-P05

## Decision: candidate

Exact identity and the full main-terminal inventory are established from
manufacturer-authored documents, including a visually inspected connection
diagram. `GV2-P05` and the North-American spelling `GV2P05` are the same catalog
item; the hyphen is a presentation separator, not a variant. No successor was
substituted.

## Identity evidence

- **Manufacturer datasheet `GV2P05`** (references/gv2p05-datasheet.pdf, dated
  25 May 2021). Title: "Motor circuit breaker, TeSys GV2, 3P, 0.63-1 A, thermal
  magnetic, screw clamp terminals". Confirms: Device short name GV2P; product
  type Circuit breaker; device application Motor; trip technology
  Thermal-magnetic; **Poles 3P**; **Control type Rotary knob** (matches source
  "operating with rotary knob"); In 1 A; **thermal adjustment 0.63…1 A** (matches
  source); magnetic tripping current 13 A; Ue/Ui 690 V AC; Ith 1 A; Icu 100 kA up
  to 690 V AC; power dissipation 2.5 W/pole; tightening torque 1.7 N·m; screw
  clamp terminals; IP20; 89 × 45 × 97 mm.
- **Schneider Digest Section 18** (references/schneider-section18.pdf,
  © 2017, 6/20/2017), Table 18.123 physical p.33: row `0.63–1` A →
  push-button `GV2ME05` / **rotary-handle `GV2P05`**, max fuse/CB 450 A. The
  TeSys GV family text (p.33) confirms 3-pole, UL 508 listed manual starter with
  manual disconnect, class-10 ambient-compensated thermal overload and
  instantaneous magnetic trip in one unit.

## Terminal / connection evidence (diagram inspected)

Datasheet **p.9 "Connections and Schema", block `GV2P••`**, rendered at 600 DPI
and read directly:

- Line side (top): **1/L1, 3/L2, 5/L3**
- Load side (bottom): **2/T1, 4/T2, 6/T3**
- Three poles, each drawn with a switching contact in series with a thermal
  (bimetal) overload element and a magnetic release (`I>`).
- A dashed line links the operating mechanism to all three poles — a common
  mechanical trip / manual-operator linkage (ganged). This is a mechanical
  interlock, **not** electrical continuity.

No protective-earth / bonding terminal appears on the device; it is a DIN-rail /
panel-screw mounted 3-pole unit. Panel or metalwork bonding is external and is
not a device connection, so no PE terminal is invented.

## Ratings — recorded as capability, not assigned intent

- Voltage capability **Ue = Ui = 690 V AC** (max, IEC 60947-2). No `nominal_voltage` is assigned to the
  terminals; the installed circuit voltage was not determined here.- Rated current In = 1 A; thermal adjustment 0.63–1 A; fixed magnetic trip 13 A.
  These conditional/adjustment figures are documentation, not automated
  conductor-sizing or trip-setting checks.

## Accessories are separate orders (not modeled)

Auxiliary contact blocks, fault/trip-signaling contacts and voltage-release trips
(GVAN / GVAE / GVAD / GVA●●● add-on blocks, one trip-or-signal contact max per
device) and the UL508 Type-E line-side barrier GV2GH7 are separately ordered
accessories with their own terminals. The source lists only the base `GV2-P05`
order and no accessory, so only the base device (6 main terminals) is modeled.

## Modeling

- 6 main terminals with verified markings; 3 poles as `contact` functions
  (symbol `breaker`), `ganged_with` relations for the mechanical link.
- The switching poles are not permanent joins: **no fixed links**; ganging is
  mechanical only.
- `connectionCoverage: complete` for the base device — its entire documented
  connection inventory is the six main terminals; note records that optional
  accessories add separate terminals.

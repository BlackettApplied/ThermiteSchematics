## Modeling

- **Purely mechanical accessory.** No electrical terminals, no pins, no PE/bond,
  no shield, no switching, no ratings are attributed to the cap by the manufacturer.
  Per the hint ("Do not guess PE on an extra-low-voltage connector") and worker
  guidance ("Mechanical caps have no invented electrical pins"), no electrical
  connections are authored.
- `conformance`: `terminalKeys: []`, `requiredTerminalKeys: []`, `fixedLinks: []`,
  `renderKind: "accessory"`.
- **Voltage independence:** the manufacturer lists one cap order number for all
  "16 A a 4 poli" connectors regardless of voltage/colour/coding. The "480–500 V"
  in the source is a property of the capped plug (`8570/12-407`), not of the cap.
- **Not the 3-pole cap.** The extra-low-voltage exclusion note applies only to the
  3-pole cap `8570001140`; it is **not** carried onto this 4-pole cap. The exact
  order number `8570002140` is preserved (no successor/abbreviation substituted).

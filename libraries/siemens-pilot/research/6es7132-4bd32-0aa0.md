## Accepted modeling scope

- Exact electronic module represented at the documented TM-E15C24-01 (6ES7193-4CB30-0AA0) assembled field interface: eight numbered field clamps and no AUX clamps. Card and base are separately ordered. Verify the installed per-slot pairing before use. Do not add this abstraction and a second full field-clamp representation to the same circuit.
- Field assignment:1=DO0,5=DO1,2=DO2,6=DO3;3/7/4/8=M. BP.L+ and BP.1M are authoring aliases for the internal functional supply interfaces, not numbered mating fingers.
- Exact p.8 Figure2-1 joins BP.1M to3/7/4/8; use explicit source jumpers. Outputs remain independent. The p.7 compatible-base column confirms24-01 has eight clamps and no AUX terminals.
- 24 V DC nominal load voltage from power module. Output rated current 2 A each, permissible ON current 7 mA-2.4 A; aggregate output current is limited to 4 A per module. Lamp load maximum 10 W. Resistive switching maximum 1 kHz; inductive 2 Hz at 0.5 H; lamp 10 Hz. No scalar current rating overrides these coupled conditions (pp.9-10).
- Parallel outputs are allowed for redundant load control within a module, not increased capacity. Mechanical application of load voltage at the power module can produce approximately 25 microseconds of ON output (pp.6,10). Circuit symbols do not simulate switching behavior.
- Only internal supply endpoints are universally required. Output channels and individual return/AUX clamps depend on the application. A fixed return link may satisfy required-terminal presence without proving external supply installation.
- The electronic card does not own the separate base clamps. Internal operating-supply endpoints are functional interfaces, not an enumeration of mating fingers. Backplane communication/contact inventory and installed per-slot base pairing remain unverified. Compatible A1-base auxiliary clamps are not part of this selected 24-01 arrangement. Preserve partial coverage; no PE, shield or AUX bond is inferred.

The compatible TM-E15C24-01 column is on p.7 of the exact manual. Optional AUX1 terminals shown in other columns belong to those different bases. The model includes only the chosen eight-clamp arrangement.

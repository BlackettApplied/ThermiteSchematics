The diagram's dashed line is **mechanical coupling between the two contacts in the double block**. It is recorded as `ganged_with` between the two contact functions. This is a fact printed on this exact block, not an inferred operating-head mechanism. The manufacturer states NC changes at **1.5 mm**, NO at **2.6 mm**, total travel **4.3 mm**; “ganged” here means mechanical coupling, not simultaneous electrical transitions. No timing simulation, external head behavior or safety assembly is claimed. Positive opening applies to the NC under the stated EN/IEC 60947-5-1 appendix K condition.

Neither the dashed mechanical line nor a normally closed contact joins physical nets. There are **no fixed links, shared electrical common or universally required terminals**. The functions use supported generic `contact-nc` and `contact-no` symbols.

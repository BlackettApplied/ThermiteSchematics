Rating and modeling boundaries

20 A is the breaker rating, not allowable conductor ampacity. IEC/EN 60898-1: 400 V AC, 50/60 Hz, Icn 10 kA, C-curve reference temperature 30 °C. The separate IEC/EN 60947-2 table gives 440 V AC, Icu 15 kA and Ics 11.2 kA for this current range, with a 55 °C C-curve reference. These conditions remain notes; no scalar breaking-capacity or assigned supply-voltage field is used. Terminal ratings retain only 20 A. DC application and coordination are not evaluated.

Two contact functions use existing breaker symbols and ganged_with metadata. Open is the diagram's depicted OFF state, not a prediction of installation state. Functions never join nets or simulate trip behavior. Coverage is complete at the manufacturer's marked electrical-terminal level; individual entry apertures are not separate endpoints.

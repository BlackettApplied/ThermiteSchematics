## Accepted model

- Assembled field-interface abstraction on documented TM-E15C24-01 (6ES7193-4CB30-0AA0): eight field clamps, no AUX clamps. Card and base are separately ordered. Verify the installed per-slot pairing before use. Do not duplicate these field clamps with a second base instance in the same circuit.
- Field assignments: 1/2 = channel 0 positive/negative; 5/6 = channel 1 positive/negative; 3/7 = current-limited transmitter L+; 4/8 = M_ANA. Exact Figure 1-1 labels functional supply P1=L+ and P2=1M.
- Exact p.8 diagram shows fixed 3–7, P2–4 and 4–8 paths. Use explicit project jumpers. Do not bridge P1 to 3 across active current limiting or bridge an input measuring impedance. No AUX field clamps exist in the chosen 24-01 arrangement.
- Rated load supply from power module: 24 VDC. Consumption maximum 30 mA; typical loss 0.6 W. Transducer supply short-circuit protection 60 mA for both channels together, not a per-terminal continuous rating. Input ranges +/-20 mA and 4-20 mA, 50 ohm input resistance; 40 mA destruction limit is not operating current. Keep these conditional values in notes.
- Channels are isolated from backplane bus, not from one another or L+. Manual pp.7,9 gives 13 bits for 4-20 mA (including overshoot at p.9). Unused channels can be deactivated (p.22), so field connections are application-dependent. P1/P2 are required supply interfaces; an internal P2 jumper can satisfy presence checking without proving an external supply return.
- Shield termination is an external shield connection bonded to the ground bus (p.22), not a numbered card terminal. Grounding and external-transmitter reference conditions in pp.19-21 remain installation responsibilities. Shared connection policy supports explicit internal jumpers, not approval for multiple field wires per clamp.
- Partial physical inventory: numbered clamps belong to the selected separate base; P1/P2 identify internal functional supplies, not every mating finger. Backplane contacts, actual per-slot pairing and external shield/bonding hardware remain unverified. No additional PE or AUX bond is inferred.

The fixed links are explicit source jumpers in the example; functions and metadata do not create continuity. Module capability ratings do not establish conductor sizing or protective coordination.

manufacturer-backed: **8570/12-407, article 150555, 16 A, 480-500 V AC,
50/60 Hz, 3P+PE, black, 7h**. The installed hardware revision remains unknown.

## Connection inventory and modeling decision

| Connection       | Cable-side endpoint | Mating endpoint | Representation                                |
| ---------------- | ------------------- | --------------- | --------------------------------------------- |
| L1 power pole    | C.L1                | M.L1            | Fixed project jumper C.L1-M.L1                |
| L2 power pole    | C.L2                | M.L2            | Fixed project jumper C.L2-M.L2                |
| L3 power pole    | C.L3                | M.L3            | Fixed project jumper C.L3-M.L3                |
| Protective earth | C.PE                | M.PE            | Fixed project jumper C.PE-M.PE; C.PE required |

L1/L2/L3 are manufacturer identities from the four-contact plug drawing.
`C` and `M` are authoring aliases distinguishing cable terminations from mating
contacts; textual `PE` stands for the earth symbol. Eight authoring endpoints
describe four through paths, not eight plug pins. Actual cable-clamp markings,
individual screw positions, extra bond points and exact 7h contact coordinates
remain unverified. No rotation of the 6h drawing, numerical pin alias, rear-view
orientation or universal mating compatibility is inferred.

Each endpoint uses a singleton bus function and the existing `terminal` circuit
symbol. Only the four explicit project jumpers join matching endpoints; bus
functions and `connectorPorts.MATE` describe contacts without producing
continuity. The jumpers represent fixed factory conductors, not added field
wires. Unlike the socket's switched poles, the plug has no modeled switches or
ganging relation. The existing `thermite:io-module` overview symbol is reused.

No neutral, shield, signal, communication, auxiliary or accessory electrical
contact is verified for this exact base plug. This does not establish the absence
of every physical screw or terminal feature. `connectionCoverage.status` is
therefore `partial`. Three power poles have 500 V AC and 16 A capability; 500 V is
the rating's upper bound, not a declaration of connected supply voltage.

The manufacturer's protective-conductor requirement is explicit on `C.PE`.
`connection_policy: shared` permits the internal-path representation plus an
external lead; it does not authorize multiple field conductors at a clamp.
**An internal PE jumper alone satisfies the compiler's presence test.** It cannot
prove external bonding or an upstream earth path. The example tests this limit
with both external PE wires removed and the fixed internal PE path retained.

## Focused verification

`examples/8570-12-407/verify.mjs --isolated` snapshots only this exact type and its
fixture library, creates a scratch byte lock, and leaves shared library locks
untouched. After integration, an ordinary run checks the tracked project lock as
well. `--output-dir <directory>` saves the unmodified generated SVG and compiler
diagnostics. The fixture has no installed supply, load, protection or conductor
size; its boundary devices are explicitly synthetic and partial.

The focused check verifies eight endpoints; four correctly paired, mutually
isolated nets; all eight boundary wires; all four explicit factory jumpers; every
function's render mapping; and the full single-sheet circuit drawing. It removes
each factory path independently to verify the corresponding open circuit and
removes all jumpers to establish that metadata cannot connect the eight endpoints.
A deliberate L1-L2 jumper is rejected by the test's isolation assertion; the
compiler is not asked to infer safety from an unenergized topology.

The missing-PE case removes the internal PE path and the cable PE lead, producing
`W903` for `XP.C.PE`. The floating-PE case keeps only the internal PE conductor;
the absence of `W903` demonstrates the presence-check limitation. A changed
library byte produces `E108`. The valid baseline preserves three `W904` warnings
(the plug and two synthetic boundaries).

The local focused check passed on Node 24.18.0. The tabloid landscape SVG was
rasterized and visually reviewed: all four paths, eight endpoints, twelve
conductors, view notes and the title block are legible without clipping. The
generated SVG is evidence, not electrical source. The shared example is locked
to the pilot library bytes; ordinary verification also checks that integrated
lock. The complete source-alpha check passed after integration.

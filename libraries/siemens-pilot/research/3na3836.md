Connection inventory: two power blade endpoints, using Siemens circuit-diagram identifiers 1 and 2. These are not claimed as stamped physical blade markings or as an installation orientation. The front indicator has no separate electrical contact in the supplied diagram; grip lugs are live handling hardware, not PE or wiring terminals. The bare link has no separately documented return, bonding, shield, signal or communication terminal. Fuse-base wiring and accessory signal detectors are outside this exact order.

Factory continuity: **no fixed links**. The fusible path between 1 and 2 is interruptible protection, not a permanent net join. Model it as one closed contact function with the supported `fuse` circuit symbol; this represents an intact link and does not simulate clearing, selectivity or let-through energy. Both passive endpoints remain application-dependent (`required: false`). Connection coverage is complete for the bare fuse link's external circuit endpoints.

Rating conditions from S2 p.1: 160 A AC rated current; listed operational currents are 156.8 A at 30 °C, 152 A at 40 °C, 147.2 A at 45 °C, 144 A at 50 °C, 140.8 A at 55 °C and 137.6 A at 60 °C. Voltage capabilities are 500 V AC / 250 V DC. Keep these in notes, with no nominal circuit voltage or unconditional scalar current rating. Ambient operation is separately listed as -5 to +40 °C on p.2; the derating table does not by itself authorize operation beyond this range. Engineering suitability is not evaluated.

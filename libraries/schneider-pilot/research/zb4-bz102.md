# zb4-bz102 — Schneider Electric ZB4-BZ102

## Identity (confirmed)

- **Order number:** ZB4-BZ102 (source/expected spelling with presentation hyphen).
  Manufacturer catalog and datasheet spell it **ZB4BZ102** (no hyphen); same part.- **Manufacturer:** Schneider Electric — range **Harmony XB4 (metal)**.
- **What it is:** _Single contact block with body/fixing collar, 1 NC, screw-clamp
  terminal._ A "body/contact assembly" sub-assembly: a single normally-closed (NC)
  contact block mounted on a Zamak (die-cast) body/fixing collar. It is **not** a
  complete operator — the head (pushbutton / selector / E-stop) is ordered
  separately and clips onto this collar.

## Terminal inventory

| Key | Function   | Marking (datasheet) | Notes                    |
| --- | ---------- | ------------------- | ------------------------ |
| 11  | NC contact | ISO n°1 (11-12) NC  | slow-break, silver alloy |
| 12  | NC contact | ISO n°1 (11-12) NC  | slow-break, silver alloy |

One slow-break NC contact = two screw-clamp terminals (11, 12). No other
electrical connection exists. The **Zamak body/fixing collar is a mechanical
mounting interface**; the datasheet's "Connections – terminals" enumerates only
the two contact terminals, so **no electrical/PE bonding terminal is modeled** and
no panel/metalwork bond is asserted.

Because this contact is rated for **both AC-15 and DC-13**, no AC-only (or DC-only)
scalar `rating` is placed on the terminals; the conditional switching table stays
in these notes (per policy: a range/table is a capability, not an assigned intent).

## Modeling decisions

- Function `nc` = contact, normal_state **closed**, terminals **[11, 12]**.
- Circuit symbol **contact-nc** (a bare NC contact block; the operating head that
  would make it a "pushbutton" is a separate order and not part of ZB4BZ102).
- No `required` terminals: a passive control contact is application-dependent.
- **No fixed links**: an NC contact is a switchable contact, not a permanent
  jumper; contact membership never joins nets.
- `connectionCoverage: complete` — the datasheet exhaustively lists the part's
  connections as the single NC contact (11-12); both are modeled. The mechanical
  collar and the separate head are outside this electrical inventory.
- `renderKind: circuit`.

## Decision

**candidate.** Exact identity confirmed against the manufacturer XB4 catalog row
and the Schneider product datasheet; terminal marking (11-12) NC and ratings taken
from the manufacturer datasheet.

## Remaining uncertainty (minor)

- The (11-12) marking is a manufacturer datasheet **text field**; no separate
  graphical contact scheme was inspected. For a single two-terminal NC contact the
  11↔12 pairing is unambiguous, so this is not material.
- The metal fixing collar's bonding to panel/metalwork is undocumented as a
  terminal and is intentionally not modeled.

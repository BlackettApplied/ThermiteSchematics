## What the part is (electrical modeling)

Interphase (inter-pole) barriers are insulating plastic separators inserted between the phases at the
breaker's power-connection terminals. Their function is to reinforce phase-to-phase and phase-to-earth
insulation by increasing creepage/clearance distance at the connections (per Schneider guidance this
type of insulation reinforcement is applied for higher operating voltages). They are **purely
mechanical/dielectric**:

- No electrical terminals, pins, screws-as-terminals, or conductive paths.
- No power, return, PE/bond, shield, signal or communication connections.
- No circuit functions and **no continuity is manufactured** — barriers only separate/insulate; they
  never join nets. Mechanical placement on a host breaker does not create any conducting path, and no
  terminals of the host breaker belong to this accessory.

Therefore the connection inventory is genuinely complete with zero electrical connections.

## Ratings

No electrical rating is assigned. This is an insulating accessory; any voltage figures in Schneider
literature describe the application condition for insulation reinforcement, not a switching/supply
rating of the barrier itself. `rating` is intentionally omitted (no `nominal_voltage`).

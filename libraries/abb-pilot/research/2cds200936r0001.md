Connection inventory and continuity: four screw/clamping terminal positions, 13, 14, 21, 22; all are passive control-contact connections and application-dependent. No universally required connection, supply, return, PE, shield or communication interface is identified for this auxiliary switch. Both contact functions share mechanical actuation; they do not share a common electrical terminal. No permanent factory links: the NC path is switched, not an unconditional net union. Complete coverage applies to this accessory alone, excluding the attached breaker and enclosure bonding. Existing contact-no/contact-nc symbols cover both functions.

Ratings: S2 section 3.3 gives AC-14: 1 A at 400 V, 2 A at 230 V; DC-12: 1 A at 220 V, 1.5 A at 110 V; DC-13: 2 A at 60 V, 4 A at 24 V. Conventional free-air thermal current is 10 A, not a universal switching rating. Conditional short-circuit withstand is 1,000 A at 230 V AC with S201 K4 protection. Impulse withstand is 4 kV (1.2/50 microseconds), category III. Maximum torque is 0.8 N m; up to two 1.5 mm² conductors. S1 also lists conductor range 0.75–2.5 mm².

Historical qualification: S2 specifies minimum 24 V AC/DC, 0.12 VA / 5 mA under its clean indoor AC-12/DC-12 conditions; S1 lists 10 mA at 12 V and 5 mA at 24 V. These differently qualified statements are retained separately. No scalar terminal rating or assigned operating voltage is modeled. No essential identity or connection uncertainty remains; suitability and as-built use are not established by this proposal.

## What it is (electrical vs. mechanical scope)

ZB4BZ009 is the **mechanical metal body / fixing collar** of the Harmony XB4 (metal,
Ø22 mm) modular system. It clamps the operator head to a Ø22 mm panel cut-out and
carries snap-in **contact blocks** (ZBE10x/20x/50x…) and **light blocks** — all of
which are **separate order numbers**. Rendered catalog p.78 (physical) shows the
collar as a bare metal carrier frame; the "Contact functions" table below it is a
distinct section for the separately ordered blocks.

Per assignment guidance, contacts/LEDs from a complete assembly
are **not** imported into this mechanical carrier, and contact/load membership never
joins nets.

## Bonding / attachment interface

The collar is metal, but the catalog documents **no PE/bonding terminal** on it. Any
metal-to-panel or metal-to-insert bond is an installation detail, not a manufacturer-
stated terminal. Following policy, this is modeled as **zero documented terminals with
partial coverage** — an assumed bonding path is not asserted as fact.

## Modeling decision

`decision: candidate`. Identity is firmly established from primary manufacturer
sources, and the electrical scope is unambiguous: a **zero-terminal mechanical
accessory**. Modeled with empty `terminals`/`functions`, `renderKind: accessory`,

collar manufactures no continuity.

# 1651330000 — Weidmüller 1651330000

Decision: **candidate, partial connection coverage**. Exact type **HDC HVE 6+2 MS** is the male screw insert, size 6. The corrected type has **17 endpoints**, **eight fixed numbered contact paths**, and **one required aggregate PE**.

## Numbering and roles

[S2, historical manufacturer HVE catalogue extract at Valin](https://files.valinonline.com/userfiles/documents/weidmuller-hve-exciting-voltage-connectors.pdf), physical **p. 4**, places the exact male screw order **1651330000** beside its numbered male arrangement. The populated positions are **1, 3, 5, 7, 10, 12, 14, 16**. The full page and a native-resolution crop were visually inspected. This is the 6+2 drawing itself, not an inferred sequence from the adjacent 3+2 model. The extract is undated; its first page is printed B.78, but p. 4 has no asserted printed number.

[S3, manufacturer RockStar catalogue 2721750000, version 2020](https://media.distributordatasolutions.com/web-weidmueller/2019q4/14e64d3d6d3f2416e981f461d312c26102460a1d.pdf), **B.84 / physical PDF 134**, lists this exact male screw order and gives the role legend. Comparing its two end switch-contact positions with S2's numbered arrangement identifies **1 and 16 as pilot/switch contacts**, and **3, 5, 7, 10, 12, 14 as operating/power contacts**. This is a **cross-diagram inference**, independently consistent with the six-power/two-signal and two-lagging descriptions in S1. The historical filled/open marks are opposite the modern convention. The modern schematic also simplifies a lateral offset at one end; no physical face-view orientation is encoded. It would be wrong to apply the modern fill legend directly to the old drawing.

[S1, exact manufacturer data sheet](https://datasheet.weidmueller.com/pdf/en/1651330000/scope/2/), creation **25.02.2026 05:57:18 MEZ**, pp. **1 and 3**, confirms the identity, male screw connection, six power contacts, two signal contacts and two lagging contacts. Pages 1, 3 and 4 were visually reviewed. The previous worker's failed download was recovered with ordinary certificate-checked system curl.

## Electrical scope

| Numbered contacts   | Role                                          | Fixed within-insert paths |
| ------------------- | --------------------------------------------- | ------------------------- |
| 1, 16               | Pilot/switch signal contacts                  | C.1–M.1, C.16–M.16        |
| 3, 5, 7, 10, 12, 14 | Operating/power contacts                      | C.n–M.n for each listed n |
| PE                  | Aggregate protective-earth authoring endpoint | None asserted             |

Numeric keys preserve the manufacturer population. `C.` means cable connection and `M.` means male mating contact; these prefixes are authoring aliases. Each numbered contact is a passive conductive path through the insert. Project links represent the eight paths explicitly; bus functions and port metadata do not join nets. No link crosses to a separately mated insert or joins different contacts. Pilot engagement does not create an internal normally-open relay or an internally closed pilot loop.

The PE clamp is documented, but all separate cable/mating/bonding interfaces are not enumerated. Thus **only `PE`** is modeled, with `required: true` as the library connection-coverage policy. There is no `C.PE`, `M.PE`, invented central pin or split-PE fixed path. Required presence does not establish external bonding. The `MATE` port lists the eight numbered contacts only and explicitly retains partial PE scope.

## Source differences retained

- **Current:** the historical p. 4 header states **23 A**, documenting a manufacturer rating. The 2020 B.80/B.84 header states **20 A**. S1 itself has **23 A** in the p. 1 introduction, **20 A** in the order summary and **20 A** in the p. 3 DIN EN 61984 field. The former claim that 23 A was uncorroborated is withdrawn. No single current is selected or explained away.
- **Voltage:** S2 p. 1 and S3 B.80 specify **500 V for signal contacts**, distinct from the **830 V** main capability; S1 gives **600 V AC/DC** under UL/CSA. These are conditional capabilities, not assigned nominal supplies. No scalar current or voltage rating is encoded.
- **Preparation:** the historical and exact sheet's screw stripping length is 9 mm; the 2020 catalogue says 8 mm. S1 also contains 2.5 and 4 mm² maxima in different fields. The type does not convert these into conductor-selection instructions.
- **Terminology:** the historical English wording about lagging earth contacts conflicts with the explicit modern signal/lagging-contact count and separate PE. It is not used to claim delayed PE engagement or safe live mating.
- **S4 customer drawing:** [exact manufacturer download](https://assets.dam.weidmueller.com/assets/api/889db943-fa52-4662-aa93-555d9b67e581/original/1651330000_HDC_HVE_6%2B2_MS_STP_Blatt__1.pdf), one page, title/order exact, drawn 24.10.2005, checked 31.10.2005. Its body drawing labels sixteen positions and its annotation states 630 V/16 A. This does not establish that sixteen contacts are populated in this eight-contact part. It is retained as conflicting construction evidence and is not used as pin-population or rating authority.

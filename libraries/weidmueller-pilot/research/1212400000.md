## Exact identity and variant

The [current manufacturer datasheet](https://datasheet.weidmueller.com/pdf/en/1212400000/scope/2/) was obtained successfully with normal TLS on 2026-09-11. Its creation stamp is **17.02.2026 01:50:39 MEZ**. Pages 1–4 were rendered and inspected visually.

- Page 1 identifies **1212400000**, **HDC 24B ABU**, GTIN **4008190083939**, installation size **8** and a standard bulkhead housing.
- Page 3 specifies the **lower part**, **without cover**, **no cable entries** and **zero cable glands**. The side-locking clamp is on the lower side and is not replaceable. The housing is die-cast aluminium with powder coating; locking elements are stainless steel and the seal is NBR.
- Pages 1 and 3 explicitly qualify **IP65 in the plugged condition**. Page 3 requires correct seating of the flat gasket during mounting. This model does not establish the protection of a bare housing or an actual assembly.
- Page 2 provides the manufacturer **limit temperature of −40 °C to 125 °C**, resolving the original research's distributor-only limitation. No voltage or current capability is authored.

## Diagram review and connection scope

The current page 1 photograph shows the open bulkhead frame, mounting feet and locking mechanisms. The page 4 drawing is a mechanical side/end dimension drawing. Its visible dimensions include the 130 mm mounting pitch, 120 mm body length, 163 mm overall span and 55 mm width. Neither this drawing nor the exact catalogue D.28 row labels a PE/bonding terminal or provides an electrical continuity diagram. Visible screws, holes and metal surfaces are not evidence of an electrical connection.

The original statement that housing earth bonding is achieved through a grounded panel and/or the insert is **unsupported and removed**. No such path is asserted in this proposal. The evidence also does not justify claiming that the actual electrical/bonding-interface inventory is known to be empty. Therefore `connectionCoverage.status` is **partial** despite the empty authored terminal inventory.

`HOUSING` is mechanical interface metadata only. No insert contacts are assigned to this part; no pin mapping, required PE endpoint or continuity through a panel, insert, screw, clamp or metalwork is invented. The separate contact insert and mating housing require their own exact-part evidence and assembly definition.

Current and historical height fields differ: the current datasheet gives overall height 29.3 mm on page 2 and housing B 30 mm on page 3; catalogue D.28 lists b = 29.5 mm. These values are preserved as source qualifications here and are not merged into an installation geometry or fit guarantee.

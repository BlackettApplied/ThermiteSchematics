## Supply, measurement and output conditions

The exact-order source keeps these circuits distinct:

- **Supply:** 220–240 VAC, 50/60 Hz; tolerance −15…+10%; 12 mA / 2.6 VA at 230 VAC; 20 ms power interruption buffering (pages 1 and 3). The voltage range is documented rather than assigned as an artificial scalar nominal voltage. Only these supply terminals receive AC-only metadata.
- **Measurement B–C:** selectable 3–30, 6–60, 30–300 or 60–600 V ranges; RMS measurement; 600 kΩ input resistance. Page 3 states 800 V pulse overload for less than 1 s and 660 V continuous capacity; these overload entries do not extend the selectable nominal ranges. Rated measurement frequency is DC or 50–60 Hz; a separate broader signal-frequency row says DC / 15 Hz–2 kHz.
- **Outputs:** two changeover poles, AgNi contacts, 250 VAC / 4 A AC maximum; AC12 4 A and AC15 3 A at 230 V; DC12 4 A and DC13 2 A at 24 V. Minimum switching values are 24 V / 10 mA, and the source specifies a maximum 10 A fast-acting fuse for NC and NO contacts (pages 3–4). Conditional contact ratings remain in notes; no single supply/measurement/output current capability is invented.
- **Construction:** PA6 housing, IP50 enclosure / IP20 terminals; 22.5 × 78 × 100 mm; DIN mounting; 8 mm conductor stripping and 0.8 N·m terminal torque; −20…+60 °C operating ambient (page 4). These are the historical order's source values.

## Operating behavior and limits of the model

The page 2 overvoltage and undervoltage timing diagrams show the **open-circuit operating principle**: the applicable threshold violation starts the configured delay, after which both output poles energize if the qualifying measurement condition persists. Delay is 0 or 0.1–30 s; hysteresis is 3–30%. The pictured NC/NO states refer to the de-energized output. This does not imply that a generic healthy condition energizes the relay.

Mode, range, threshold, hysteresis and delay are field settings, not encoded by the order code. The type provides terminal and switched-contact semantics; it does not simulate measurement, timing or site fault behavior and does not supply permanent net joins.

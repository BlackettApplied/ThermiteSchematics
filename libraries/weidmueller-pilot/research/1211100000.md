## Corrected connection inventory

| Model endpoints | Meaning                                                             | Fixed continuity                           |
| --------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------ |
| C.1–C.24        | Cable-side authoring endpoints for the 24 numbered screw contacts   | Each C.n connects only to its matching M.n |
| M.1–M.24        | Mating-side authoring endpoints for the same numbered male contacts | 24 explicit fixed paths in total           |
| PE              | Aggregate protective-earth assembly                                 | No invented C.PE–M.PE link                 |

This is **49 logical endpoints and 24 numbered fixed links**, not 49 physical pins. The contact drawings establish the labels; passive screw-contact construction supports the continuity interpretation. No electrical continuity is created by connector metadata or a bus function. The integrating fixture must instantiate the declared links.

The drawings and exact product photo show earth-associated hardware at the insert ends. They do not inventory all PE clamps, mating areas and mounting/bonding interfaces separately. Removed the unsupported central PE description and the invented single split-side PE pair. The aggregate PE key and C/M prefixes are authoring conventions; numbered labels 1–24 come directly from the male drawing. No coordinates or installed mating orientation are encoded.

PE.required is **library policy for the modeled protective-earth use**, not a universal manufacturer quotation. A shared endpoint allows electrical graph edges; it does not approve multiple conductors under one physical clamp. Presence diagnostics cannot prove external bonding, enclosure bonding, or hardware continuity. Numbered paths are application-dependent. Coverage remains **partial**.

## Ratings and unresolved installation conditions

The exact datasheet gives DIN EN 61984 **500 V / 16 A**, impulse **6 kV**, pollution severity **3**, and a separate UL/CSA voltage capability of **600 V AC/DC**. All nominal_voltage and voltage_type capability scalars have been removed: compiler E302 uses nominal voltage as exact net compatibility, not a maximum voltage capability. Retained contact current 16 means the DIN rating only; it is not an unconditional installed ampacity or a universal UL/CSA rating.

The exact datasheet p. 3 lists approval-dependent current values:

| AWG | UR current (A) | cUR current (A) |
| --- | -------------- | --------------- |
| 12  | 20             | 19.7            |
| 14  | 15             | 15              |
| 16  | 10             | 11.3            |
| 18  | 7              | 10.3            |
| 20  | 5              | 8               |

Its main connection table ends at **AWG 14**, despite AWG 12 appearing in the approval-current table. That discrepancy is not resolved by the model. Catalogue 2028800000 B.26 also shows current depending on conductor size, loaded contact count and temperature; the curves are not replaced with a universal scalar.

Main connections: **M3**, **0.5–2.5 mm² / AWG 20–14**, **9 mm** strip. Separate PE data: **M4**, **4 mm²** rated cross-section, **AWG 20–12**, **10 mm** strip, **1.2–1.5 Nm**. These are distinct termination specifications, not a count of physical clamps.

The **2026 exact datasheet** specifies main torque **0.6–0.7 Nm**. The linked **2014/2015 HE torque sheet** specifies **0.5–0.55 Nm**; both give PE **1.2–1.5 Nm**. Preserve the dated values and resolve the installed hardware revision before selecting torque.

Remaining limits: individual PE interfaces, installed accessory/housing connections, physical orientation, actual conductor assignments, appropriate approval/derating conditions and as-built bonding. No separate neutral, shield, communication or auxiliary terminal is evidenced. Housing/hood, ingress/Ex suitability and a complete mating assembly are outside scope.

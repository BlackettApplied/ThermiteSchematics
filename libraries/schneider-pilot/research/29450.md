## What 29450 is

Standard **field-installable auxiliary/indication contact**: a single
change-over (SPDT) micro-switch, catalog "signal contacts composition: 1 NO/NC".
The **slot where it is fitted defines its function** (instruction sheet §3, §5):

- OF = open/closed (ON/OFF) status of the main contacts
- SD = tripped / not-tripped (mechanical or any trip) status
- SDE = tripped due to an electrical fault (needs actuator LV429451 on some frames)
- SDV = tripped due to an earth-leakage fault (VigiPacT add-on)
- (CAO/CAF/CAM early-operation positions also exist on some frames)

The same physical part is 29450 (spring/"screwless" terminals) or 29452 (screw
terminals, "low level"). The instruction sheet warns the terminal **positions
differ** between 29450 and 29452.

## Terminal identity (visually inspected — diagramReviewed)

Rendered and read instruction-sheet pages 4, 7, 9, 13, 14 as images.

- The bare 29450 contact carries **three intrinsic terminal markings: 1
  (common, drawn with a "C"), 2, and 4** (page 4 / page 9 "4.1 29450: Auxiliary
  Contact with Spring Terminals").
- Bare contact reference: **1 common,2 NC/break,4 NO/make** (p.9). The installed
  function has a different actuation condition for each slot (section8 pp.14–15):
  OF transfers from1–2 to1–4 on breaker closure; SD transfers on trip, while either
  normal open or closed remains1–2; SDE transfers on electrical trip; SDV transfers
  on earth-leakage trip. A mechanical trip alone does not transfer SDE/SDV.
  These are distinct conditions, not interchangeable ON/OFF indications.
  Static contact functions do not simulate the breaker mechanism.
- "Accessory Label" §7 (page 13) shows the function-dependent renumbering that
  the shipped label applies by slot:
  - OF1 → 11/12/14, OF2 → 21/22/24, OF3 → 31/32/34, OF4 → 41/42/44
  - SD → 91/92/94 (drawn "AL"), SDE → 81/82/84, SDV → 71/72/74
    (pattern: common `x1`, NC `x2`, NO `x4`). The library type is modelled on the
    bare part's intrinsic markings **1/2/4**; the two-digit numbers are applied
    labels that depend on the installed slot, not separate physical terminals.

No permanent conducting jumper exists inside the part: the common (1) is joined
to 2 or to 4 only through the breaker-driven mechanism. Therefore **no fixed
links** are asserted. There is no PE, coil, supply or communication terminal.

## Ratings (conditional — kept as notes, no scalar terminal rating)

From the Schneider 29450 product data sheet (p.1). The contact is rated for
**both AC and DC**, so no single scalar/AC-only value is placed on the terminals.

- [Ith] conventional free-air thermal current: **6 A**
- Minimum load: **100 mA at 24 V**
- [Ie] rated operational current (selected rows):
  - AC-12: 6 A at 24/48/110/220–240/380–440/480/660–690 V AC (50/60 Hz)
  - AC-15: 5 A @110 V, 4 A @220/240 V, 2 A @380/440 V, 0.1 A @660/690 V
  - DC-12: 6 A @24 V, 2.5 A @48 V, 0.6 A @110 V, 0.3 A @250 V
  - DC-14: 1 A @24 V, 0.2 A @48 V, 0.05 A @110 V, 0.003 A @250 V

## Source-vs-manufacturer notes / discrepancies

- **Terminal type**: the current manufacturer instruction sheet documents 29450
  as **screwless spring** terminals (0.5–1.5 mm² / 20–16 AWG) and 29452 as screw.
  An RS Components reseller listing labels "Merlin Gerin 29450" as a _screw_-
  terminal contact; this is not corroborated by a manufacturer source. The
  electrical change-over model (1/2/4) is unaffected by clamp technology.
- **Range wording**: the source cites the legacy "Compact NS100..630" and also
  "NSX100...630". The Schneider data sheet lists the range as ComPacT NSX
  100–250/400–630, CVS, INS, INV, NG160, NR, NSC100; the instruction sheet also
  covers ComPacT NS630b–3200. The exact fit onto the legacy Merlin-Gerin Compact
  NS100–630 frame is not separately itemised in the opened docs, but the order
  code, "1 NO/NC" composition and OF/SD/SDE/SDV function set match exactly.
- The source schematic pages that would show which function (OF/SD/SDE/SDV) and
  which applied label numbering each installed `+..-F480-x` / `-811F1` /
  `-812F1` instance uses were not provided; the type models the bare contact.

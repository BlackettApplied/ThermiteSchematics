# zb4-bv013 — Schneider Electric ZB4-BV013

## Identity

- **Expected type:** `schneider-pilot:zb4-bv013`, manufacturer "Schneider Electric",
  order number `ZB4-BV013`.
- **Manufacturer spelling:** Schneider spells the code **without** the hyphen —
  `ZB4BV013` — in the catalog rows and at `se.com/product/ZB4BV013`. The source's
  `ZB4-BV013` carries a presentation hyphen only. Identity preserved as expected.

## What the part is (decision: candidate)

`ZB4BV013` is a **head-only sub-assembly** of the Harmony XB4 metal range:
a **white, plain lens + Ø22 mm metal bezel** — the optical/mechanical cap of a
pilot light. It is **not** a complete pilot light and contains **no LED, no
contacts and no electrical terminals**.

Evidence (official catalog _Harmony XB4 metal control and signaling units_,
`DIA5ED2121212EN`, October 2021 – V13.0; fetched from se.com iPortal):

- **Heads only** table, PDF p.69 (printed 67): row _"With plain lens / White =
  `ZB4BV013`"_, 0.026/0.057 kg. Table footnote (1): _"For use with bodies
  comprising a light source with integral LED only."_ Visually rendered and read
  — the entries are lens caps; no terminals, no connection diagram exists.
- **Complete-unit composition**, PDF p.68 (printed 66): the complete 24 V white
  unit `XB4BVB1 = ZB4BVB1 (body) + ZB4BV013 (head)`; likewise `XB4BVG1`
  (110–120 V) and `XB4BVM1` (230–240 V) each pair a voltage-specific body with the
  same white head `ZB4BV013`. This confirms `ZB4BV013` is only the head portion.
- **Sub-assembly overview**, PDF p.21 (printed 18): _Head sub-assemblies_
  (`ZB4BV0p3`, "with lens fitted") are listed separately from _Body sub-assemblies
  with integral LED_ which carry the **screw-clamp terminals** and the LED. p.24
  footnote refers to _"the body (fixing collar and contact)"_ — the **fixing
  collar belongs to the body**, not the head.
- **se.com product page** (`https://www.se.com/us/en/product/ZB4BV013/`):
  _"Head for pilot light, Harmony XB4, metal, white, 22 mm, universal LED, plain
  lens"_; lists no electrical connection, voltage or wiring; states a compatible
  universal LED block must be ordered separately.

### Exact-suffix discipline

- `ZB4BV013` = plain lens (this part).
- `ZB4BV013E` = plain lens **for insertion of legend** (different part).
- `ZB4BV013S` = **grooved** lens (different part).
  The source _"With plain lens, White"_ matches the plain, non-legend, non-grooved
  white head exactly. Color digit `1` = white (3 green, 4 red, 5 orange, 6 blue).

## Electrical vs mechanical scope

- **Electrical scope of ZB4BV013: none.** Zero terminals. The integral LED and the
  screw-clamp supply terminals live in the **separately ordered body sub-assembly**
  (ZB4BVB1 / ZB4BVG1 / ZB4BVM1 …), not in this head.
- **Bonding/attachment:** no electrical bond is documented on the head. The metal
  bezel snap-fits onto the body; the panel-clamping **fixing collar is part of the
  body**. No bonding pin or panel-bond path is asserted for this head.
- Therefore modeled as a **zero-terminal accessory** (documentation view), with
  no invented pins, no LED/contact terminals imported from the complete assembly,
  and no fixed links.

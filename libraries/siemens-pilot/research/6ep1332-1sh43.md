Connection specification: L, N, two positive and two negative output screws. Model aliases `+.1`, `+.2`, `-.1`, `-.2` distinguish repeated legends without guessing positions. Family manual uses L1; exact datasheet uses L, retained in model. Same-polarity screws serve one output pole: factory links `+.1–+.2` and `-.1–-.2` are the electrical interpretation of S1 p.3/S2 p.10, supported by the single isolated converter in p.13; no detailed copper-layout drawing was obtained. No primary/secondary, opposite-polarity or earth continuity. Conditional external output bonding in S2 p.25 is separate.

Rated input 100–240 V AC (operating 85–264 V, 47–63 Hz) or 110–300 V DC. Output 24 V DC, adjustment 22.2–26.4 V, aggregate 2.5 A / typical 60 W. Derating 2%/K above +55 through +70 °C; natural-convection operating range −20 to +70 °C. No duplicated 2.5 A budgets. L/N required for powered operation; individual output takeoffs application-dependent. No-load operation allowed. No PE, auxiliary, signal, shield or communication terminals; LED/potentiometer are local indications/controls.

Access limitations: Siemens support PDF URL `https://support.industry.siemens.com/cs/attachments/48565267/LOGOPower_Handbuch_englisch_en-US.pdf` failed web access (403) and local TLS verification; cache-host attempts also failed. Independent coordinator review confirmed the six-screw inventory against the exact datasheet. Additional application qualifications: standard thermal ratings assume vertical mounting, terminals uppermost and 20 mm free space above/below (manufacturer manual pp.19–20); other orientations require derating. A conditional Class I output-minus-to-PE bond uses at least 1.5 mm² (p.25), and is not an internal device connection. Rated 2.5 A is not a protection threshold: datasheet p.2 specifies typical current limiting 3.3 A and maximum enduring short-circuit RMS current 4.8 A.

# 28501 — Murr 28501 MCVO proportional amplifier

Decision: **candidate with partial connection coverage**. The exact legacy
manufacturer manual establishes eleven assembled field clamps, including four
separate returns marked `0`. Full individual return commoning and interfaces
exposed by disassembly remain unresolved. The source's Murr name, order `28501`
and type ID `murr-pilot:28501` are preserved.

## Exact identity and terminal evidence

The [manufacturer operating-instruction archive](https://shop.murrelektronik.com/out/media/28501_ina_x_11.zip)
contains `28501_ina_en_11.pdf`, **28501 V1.1, 24.02.2022**, and the German
`28501_ina_de_11.pdf`, **202 28 501 V1.1, 29.03.2022**. Both English pages were
rendered and visually inspected. Page 2 §3 gives the exact terminal arrangement
and detailed block circuit; §4 shows the alternative command connections.

The [historical manufacturer catalog at Valex](https://valex-electro.ru/public/files/murr_catalog/1_4.pdf),
physical p.6 / printed 1.4.6, independently identifies exact **28501** in the
right-hand 24 V column. The adjacent 12 V variant is not substituted. There is no
printed revision; PDF creation 2002-11-08 and modification 2003-02-20 are file
metadata, not certified publication dates.

The [manufacturer old/new article index at Audin](https://www.audin.fr/pdf/documentations/murrelektronik/3_-_connecteurs-passif-vers-le-terrain/Equivalence-ArtNr-anciens-nouveaux.pdf),
physical p.3 / printed 1.11.3, has a visually inspected **28501 →
7000-31261-0000000** row. Its metadata dates are 2004-04-07 / 2008-04-25, with no
printed revision. The [current manufacturer product page](https://shop.murrelektronik.com.cn/en/Electronics-in-the-Control-Cabinet/Active-Interface-Technology/Accessories/PROPORTIONAL-AMPLIFIER-SNAP-ON-7000-31261-0000000.html)
lists the recovered exact 28501 instruction archive. The renumbering bridge
explains that association; physical terminal marks come from the legacy manual.

## Eleven physical clamps

Exact manual p.2, front view as drawn, each row left to right:

| Row            | Printed marks         | Model keys                                     |
| -------------- | --------------------- | ---------------------------------------------- |
| Upper, valve   | `PE`, `2-`, `1+`      | `PE-out`, `2-`, `1+`                           |
| Middle, supply | `PE`, `24V`, `0`, `0` | `PE-in`, `24V`, `0-supply-a`, `0-supply-b`     |
| Lower, control | `0`, `IN`, `10V`, `0` | `0-signal-left`, `IN`, `10V`, `0-signal-right` |

The qualifiers on duplicate `0` and `PE` keys are authoring aliases, not additional
stamped labels. The compact catalog diagram's `OUT` means the reference function;
the actual manual layout marks it **10V**. Valve polarity is explicitly **1+ / 2−**.
Neither mark is guessed from a modern photograph. Front `Imin`/`Imax` and underside
`f`/`tR` are four adjustment potentiometers, not extra field terminals.

`24V` and one representative supply return are required for powered use.
`PE-in` is required by explicit library policy for modeled protective bonding;
the manual does not make that precise representative clamp universally mandatory.
Remaining clamps are application-dependent. An internal PE link can satisfy a
presence diagnostic without proving that an external earth conductor is present.

## Factory continuity and limits

Two fixed paths are evidenced: the exact legacy catalog's right-hand circuit
joins its two supply `0` interfaces, and the exact manual p.2 directly joins the
two PE interfaces. Both links are specified in `result.json` conformance for
materialization by the coordinator. Functions and symbols alone do not join nets.

The manual layout shows four `0` clamps but the detailed circuit collapses returns
to one `0` reference. The historical diagram routes a control `0` into the
converter separately. Individual physical bonds between the control returns and
the supply pair are not traced explicitly; additional return links are therefore
left unmodeled. **This is incomplete connectivity evidence, not a claim of
electrical or galvanic isolation.**

The detailed manual places **R sens. between `0` and `2-`**. No fixed link crosses
that measuring resistor. Likewise no permanent path crosses reverse-polarity
protection, PWM electronics, the converter or recovery diode. The model does not
short `24V` to `1+`, `1+` to `2-`, or `10V` to supply. Generic interface/earth
symbols describe the field functions without simulating electronic transfer
behavior.

## Optional command arrangements

Exact manual p.2 §4 distinguishes three alternatives:

- External 0–10 V command: `IN` and the illustrated lower right `0`, with `10V`
  left open.
- External potentiometer: at least 2 kΩ across `10V` and the illustrated lower
  left `0`, wiper to `IN`; reference load at most 5 mA.
- Internal parameter setting: installer-supplied `IN`–`10V` jumper. It is not a
  factory link.

`10V` is an optional generated output and must not be externally powered.
Unipolar command and operating-supply polarity must be observed despite limited
protection circuitry.

## Conditional ratings and differences

Exact manual p.1 gives supply **18–32 V DC**, maximum 10% ripple and **2200 µF
buffer capacitance per ampere of load**, approximately 10 s start-up and at most
50 mA own consumption. The supply range has no exact `nominal_voltage` scalar.
Input is 0–10 V unipolar, at least 200 kΩ. Generated reference is **+10 V ±5%,
maximum 5 mA at 24 V supply**; its nominal 10 V scalar describes that actual
reference output only.

Valve output is **PWM, operating supply minus at most 1.2 V**, with 2.5 A maximum
inductive capability. It is not an unconditional 24 V source. The 2022 manuals
give `Imax` adjustment 0.2 A to 2.5 A +10%, factory 1 A; `Imin` factory about
10 mA, adjustable to 30–40% of `Imax`. Frequency is 40 Hz −5% to 400 Hz +10%,
factory 400 Hz. Ramp-up/down are set together, 0.2 s +20% to 10 s +20%, factory
minimum. Short-circuit tolerance is temporary, at most two seconds; shutdown for
excess temperature/overload does not establish unlimited protection.

Do not silently reconcile source revisions: historical catalog `Imax` minimum
0.02 A +20% differs from the manuals' 0.2 A. Catalog/current temperature is
−20…+60°C; manual says −25…+60°C. Catalog dimensions 75 × 22.5 × 102 mm differ
from the manual's 75 × 85 × 22.5 mm with different dimension labels. Manual IP20
and 1.5 mm² clamp specification do not provide a detailed conductor-count or
preparation table.

## Coverage and comparison provenance

The proposal is partial because individual return bonds, disassembled mating
contacts and any rail-bonding interface are not exhaustively established. No
hidden pin inventory or automatic DIN-rail bond is invented.

The author reached terminal `completed` before comparison. Its original ten-pin
model used the modern generated sheet's simplified circuit, inferred grouping,
omitted one control return, missed the output polarity, and assigned exact 24 V
to both operating input and PWM output. This proposal replaces those claims
with the exact manual and changes coverage from complete to partial. The
original research's trailing XML is absent from this proposal.

Original job/schema are copied byte-identically and all five original artifact
hashes are preserved in the reviewer directory. Original acquisition attempts
remain archived: the author opened its Italian modern sheet and reported
unhelpful catalog searches and unavailable distributor pages. Independent
review recovered the exact legacy catalog, article index and manufacturer manual.
The current generic photo has fourteen numbered terminals; the old catalog's
photo visibly says 12V. Neither is used for exact 28501 field inventory.

Source downloads, full-page renders, diagram crops and hashes remain under
`reviewer-0155`, outside the proposal. No original, engine or canonical files were
changed. Formatting, central verification and promotion belong to the coordinator.

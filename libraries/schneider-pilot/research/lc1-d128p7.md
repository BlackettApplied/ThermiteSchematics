# lc1-d128p7 — Schneider Electric LC1-D128P7

## Identity

- **Manufacturer:** Schneider Electric.
- **Family:** TeSys D / TeSys Deca, non-reversing 4-pole load-control contactor.

## What the part is

`LC1D128` = 4-pole contactor, **2 NO + 2 NC power poles**, AC-1 rated **25 A**
(θ ≤ 60 °C), screw-clamp terminals, with **integrated 1 NO + 1 NC** instantaneous
auxiliary contacts. Coil suffix **`P7` = 230 V a.c. 50/60 Hz**.

## Ratings (conditional — kept out of scalar terminal fields)

- **Power poles:** AC-1, Ue ≤ 440 V, Ie 25 A (θ ≤ 60 °C); listed thermal current
  Ith 20 A on the product page. TeSys D poles are rated across AC and DC
  utilisation categories and a voltage range, so no single `nominal_voltage`/
  `voltage_type` scalar is assigned to the poles — ≤440 V is a capability ceiling,
  not an assigned supply. Screw-clamp terminals.
- **Auxiliary contacts (13/14, 21/22):** control contacts, application-dependent
  (AC-15/DC-13 etc.); no scalar rating asserted.
- **Coil A1/A2:** `P7` = **230 V a.c., 50/60 Hz**. This is a genuine nominal, so
  it is the only rating carried on a terminal (`nominal_voltage: 230`, `AC`).
  Per catalog page B8/12, within the a.c. range only **LC1D115 and D150** coils
  carry a built-in suppression device as standard; **LC1D128 P7 has no built-in
  suppressor** and, being an a.c. coil, no polarity. DC (`…D`) coils would have an
  integral diode — not this part.

## Continuity / net notes

- All contacts (power poles + auxiliaries) are **switched**; the coil is a
  separate winding. There are **no permanent internal wire joins** → `fixedLinks`
  is empty. The coil actuating the contacts is mechanical/functional
  (`internal_relations: actuates`), not electrical continuity.
- **No dedicated PE terminal** on the device body; panel/rail earthing is external
  and outside this type. Front/side add-on auxiliary blocks, timers, interface and
  suppressor modules are **separately ordered parts** and are not included here.

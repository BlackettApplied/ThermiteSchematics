## Modeling

This exact insert has six numbered power contacts. `C.1` through `C.6` are
cable-side authoring aliases; `M.1` through `M.6` are mating-side aliases. Six
explicit fixed links join the corresponding ends of each conductive contact.
The prefixes do not claim markings on the installed hardware. Functions and
connector metadata never conduct on their own.

One aggregate `PE` endpoint represents the documented protective-earth assembly.
The source does not establish a central PE pin or exactly one cable/mating PE
pair, so individual PE interfaces and an internal PE link are not invented.
`PE.required` is an explicit library policy for modeled protective-earth use,
not a quoted universal installation instruction. A connection-presence check
does not establish an upstream bond or a verified housing connection.

Coverage remains **partial**: the six numbered contact paths are documented,
but the PE assembly's individual interfaces and installed orientation are not.
IEC 400 V / 35 A and UL/CSA 600 V AC/DC describe qualified component capability;
no supply voltage is assigned. The model omits `nominal_voltage` and leaves
`voltage_type` unset. Terminal symbols expose each authored endpoint.

## Unresolved / caveats

- Exact physical position of each numbered contact (geometry of 1…6 and PE) is from the
  "Assignment 1-6" text + arrangement photo; no legible numbered diagram or orientation coords.
- Mating partner (male 1498700000, or the 7–12 variant 1498800000), housing/frame, seals and
  installed IP rating are application-dependent and unverified.
- AC vs DC service and per-contact net/phase mapping require application review; the datasheet gives only generic 1–6 numbering.
- Installed as-built hardware revision unverified.

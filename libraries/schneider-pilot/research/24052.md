## Connection and continuity specification

Inventory is complete for the bare order: two main tunnel terminals, manufacturer markings `1` and `2`. No additional neutral, PE, shield, communication or signal terminals are part of the depicted bare device. Separate auxiliaries and comb busbars are excluded. One breaker function uses the existing `breaker` circuit mark. Neither terminal is universally required for every instance; application wiring determines use.

Factory permanent links: **none**. The 1–2 path is interruptible; it must never merge the two circuit nets. The model shows the catalog open/OFF state and does not simulate thermal or magnetic protection.

## Ratings and limitations

S1 p.55 specifies nominal current at 30 °C and B magnetic trip at 3–5 × In. For C60N 1P, IEC 60947-2 Icu is 20 kA at 130 V, 10 kA at 240 V, and 3 kA at 415 V; the last is specifically the IT-system double-fault condition. S2 p.42 lists 15 kA DC breaking capability below 60 V for one pole in the C60N 6–63 A range, with magnetic uprating coefficient 1.38. General family voltage headlines are not single-pole assigned voltages. Only the 20 A device current rating is structured; voltage and conditional capacities remain notes.

S1 p.55 gives 16 mm² flexible / 25 mm² rigid cable capability up to 25 A; S2 p.42 gives a generic 35 mm² entry below 63 A. This detail differs in presentation and is not turned into an automated wiring limit. No disagreement in exact identity or pole count was found. Suitability, coordination and installed accessories are not established by this proposal.

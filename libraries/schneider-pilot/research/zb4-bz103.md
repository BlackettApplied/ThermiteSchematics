## What is included

The exact [Schneider ZB4BZ103 current product sheet](https://www.se.com/au/en/product/download-pdf/ZB4BZ103), printed **12 September 2026**, is 8 physical pages. Page 1 identifies a complete body/contact assembly, **two standard slow-break NO contacts**, screw-clamp connections and Zamak fixing collar. This agrees with the [October 2021 Harmony XB4 catalog](https://iportal.se.com/Contents/docs/HARMONY%20XB4%20METAL%20CONTROL%20AND%20SIGNALING%20UNITS_CATALOG.PDF), **printed 76 / physical 78**, whose exact ZB4BZ103 row specifies two NO contacts with body/fixing collar. The head is a separately selected part. Neither an operating head, lamp, coil nor extra contact block is included here.

The source spelling **ZB4-BZ103** is preserved in the catalog identity. Opened Schneider sources spell it **ZB4BZ103**; this candidate associates the identical alphanumeric order and exact two-NO/collar composition as a documented punctuation normalization. No suffixed replacement was substituted. A manufacturer sentence explicitly equating those punctuation forms was not retrieved. Historical installed markings remain unverified. Search-index excerpts for historical hyphenated manufacturer catalogs were found, but their Construmatica host could not be resolved/opened; those excerpts are not treated as opened source proof.

## Physical terminals and aliases

The current sheet's exact product photos on **physical pages 1, 7 and 8** were visually inspected. The side photograph identifies one fitted block as **ZBE-101** and shows its **NO 3–4 circuit diagram**. Crucially, the **page 8 rear photograph independently shows both fitted blocks**: each has **NO**, **3** and **4** markings adjacent to its own two screw clamps. The four visible electrical points establish both pairs directly; no second pair is inferred from a neighboring variant.

| ----------- | -------------- | --------------------------------- |
| B1.3        | 3              | Photo-left NO block, upper clamp  |
| B1.4        | 4              | Photo-left NO block, lower clamp  |
| B2.3        | 3              | Photo-right NO block, upper clamp |
| B2.4        | 4              | Photo-right NO block, lower clamp |

**B1/B2 are library qualifiers, not manufacturer marks.** The reference is the page 8 rear-facing photo, oriented with the central collar fastening screw at the bottom and the terminal 3 labels above the green actuators. This defines the photograph's left/right blocks only; it does not assign an installed panel orientation or selector position. Each pair is a separate NO function, open in its unactuated state. Whether the blocks actuate together or separately depends on the separately specified head, so no ganged relation or head actuator function is asserted.

## Earlier evidence and remaining boundaries

The [2022 exact sheet](https://cdn-reichelt.de/documents/datenblatt/C300/ZB4BZ103_DB-EN.pdf), **4 October 2022, physical page 1**, already showed the visible block's 3–4 NO diagram. Its abbreviated **(13-14)NO** ISO field omitted the second contact. The current sheet retains that field, but its rear photo supplies all actual clamp markings. The ISO field is preserved as an incomplete documentary description, not modeled as extra physical terminals.

[Schneider FAQ FA23069](https://www.se.com/au/en/faqs/FA23069/), published 21 October 2010, modified 2 March 2026, names the ZB4BD3 + ZB4BZ103 selector assembly. Its inspected application diagram labels the two S2 circuits **13–14 and 23–24**. The FAQ does not map those circuit designations to the physical photo-defined B1/B2 blocks. No such mapping is needed for this isolated body/contact model, and none is asserted. The FAQ's conductors are external application wiring, not internal links.

- **No fixed links:** neither contact membership nor the common mechanical collar joins electrical nets.
- **No required terminals:** these passive contacts are application-dependent.
- **Partial coverage:** all four contact clamps are represented, but fixing-collar/panel bonding and any dedicated electrical attachment interface are undocumented. The mechanical fastening screw is not invented as a PE terminal or automatic panel bond.
- **Ratings remain conditional notes:** Ith 10 A, Ui 600 V at pollution degree 3, Uimp 6 kV. Ie is AC-15/A600 6 A at 120 V, 3 A at 240 V, 1.2 A at 600 V; DC-13/Q600 0.55 A at 125 V, 0.27 A at 250 V, 0.1 A at 600 V. No scalar nominal voltage or universal 10 A switching limit is assigned.

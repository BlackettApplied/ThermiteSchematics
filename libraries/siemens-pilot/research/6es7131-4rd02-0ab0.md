## Ratings, disagreement and limits

The historical description's “24Vdc” agrees with module supply in S2; it must not be assigned to the NAMUR sensor terminals. S1 pp.15–16 gives NAMUR signal 0 at 0.35–1.2 mA and signal 1 at 2.1–7 mA, electronic sensor-supply short-circuit protection and no additional redundant sensor supply. These are conditional operating facts, not scalar terminal current limits. No voltage or current scalar is encoded.

S1 p.15 specifies maximum input delay 4.6 ms; S2 p.2 prints 4.6 µs. S1 p.15 gives two address bytes; S2 p.1 gives four packed bits or one unpacked byte. These manufacturer-document discrepancies remain unresolved and are not modeled.

## Electrical model

A DIN NH gG fuse link is a **two-terminal** series element: two knife-blade
contacts, one at each end. There are **no marked terminal numbers** on an NH
fuse link, so the two blades are declared with functional aliases `1` and `2`
(explicitly aliases, not manufacturer markings). The element is non-polarized /
bidirectional — the alias ordering carries no orientation.

- One function `element` — `kind: contact`, `normal_state: closed` (a healthy
  fuse conducts) over terminals `1`–`2`, circuit symbol `fuse`.
- **No fixed link.** A fuse is not a permanent conductor; it normally conducts
  but must not permanently join the two nets (per job/WORKER guidance).
- No auxiliary contacts, no striker/microswitch terminal (the blown-fuse
  indicator on the standard gG link is **visual only**; the OFM/OFS/OFD fuse
  monitors with 11/14/21/22 aux contacts are separate accessory parts and are
  **not** part of this link).
- No PE / bond terminal.

`connectionCoverage: complete` — the two blade contacts are the entire
electrical interface of the link. Blade terminal _markings_ are unverified
(none exist on NH links); the _inventory_ is complete.
